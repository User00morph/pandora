When we talk about consciousness, most people think about their thoughts. What consciousness is actually much bigger than that. A huge part of consciousness is the orientation from which you experience reality, time and space. Your memories of the past, your interpretation of the present, your expectations about the future and your beliefs about yourself and the world, those represent your coordinates. All of that creates the internal position from which you perceive and act. That's why two people can live through the exact same situation and experience completely different realities. Not because the situation is different, the situation is the same. But because their orientation inside consciousness is different. And the interesting part is that most of that, if not all of it, orientation is invisible. You can't see someone's beliefs. You can't see their internal stories or narratives. You can't see the assumptions shaping their reaction, their reactions. But you can see the effects. You can see their patterns in their behavior. You can see their emotional reactions. You can see the choices that they make again and again. So consciousness isn't just awareness. It's partly the unseen architecture, organizing how you experience reality across time. And this is exactly where coherence comes in. coherence is alignment between your thoughts, your feelings and your actions. It's the process of bringing that unseen orientation or internal architecture into alignment. Because when your thoughts and your feelings and your actions are pulling you in different directions, your life feels chaotic. But when they align, your internal orientation becomes much clearer. And once that happens, you're able to go from being a reactor to your life to actually authoring how you move through it.
