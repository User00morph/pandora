## EXTENSION 3 — ACCOUNTING FRAMEWORK (Deploy — knowledge layer)

**New. Use immediately to reframe disputes and negotiations.**

### The Leverage Point
Credit bureau reports are NOT certified financial statements. They are:
- Not audited
- Not signed by a CPA
- Not prepared under GAAP

All US public companies and banks must follow GAAP per **12 USC 18(2)**. This means any creditor operating under public accounting rules cannot produce a certified financial statement from a credit bureau report. This is the legal gap through which debt disputes enter.

### When Disputing Any Account
1. Request certified financial statements from the creditor (audited, CPA-signed)
2. They cannot produce them for credit-bureau-level reporting
3. Request a securitization audit / Bloomberg report on the note
4. If securitized: original lender no longer holds the note (UCC Art. 3 → Art. 8 conversion)
5. File complaint as private investor (not consumer) — different legal posture, more protections

### Admin Process Ladder (for any debt/account dispute)
```
1. File complaint directly with creditor (certified mail, affidavit format, 10–21 days)
2. Send W9 requesting their EIN — requires certified affidavit response (they won't)
3. Form 3949-A → IRS (triggers audit on the counterparty)
4. SEC investor complaint (private investor status)
5. FINRA arbitration (most credit agreements have arbitration clauses)
6. FTC complaint
7. State Attorney General if fraud provable
8. Federal district court if state court commits fraud on the record
```

Come in as: **private investor / private banker**
Not as: consumer seeking remedy under consumer law
