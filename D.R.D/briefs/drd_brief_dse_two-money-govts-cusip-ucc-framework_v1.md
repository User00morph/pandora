**What equitable liens are:**
- Court-recognized liens that arise from equity principles — not statute, not contract
- Exist in: trust fund interests, property transactions, service agreements
- Key cases:
  - *Re Stucley* [1906]: vendor of reversionary interest in trust = equitable lien in subject matter

  - *Langen and Wing v Bell* [1972]: service agreement + share assignment = equitable lien over transferred shares
