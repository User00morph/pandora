### FINDING 5 — CONSCIENCE DEFINED (D.P.S.A Core Concept)

**Conscience is knowledge** — not a feeling, not a behavior, not a spiritual gift:
- Latin: *con* (with) + *scire* (to know) = "to know with"
- Conscience is the capacity to know right from wrong
- It is the prison door key
- Common sense = conscience applied in daily life

**What blocks conscience:** Moral relativism — the belief that right and wrong don't exist. This is the system's philosophy because it prevents conscience from functioning.

**D.P.S.A practice:** Regularly ask: "Do I know what the right thing is here?" If yes → do it. The gap between knowing and doing is the conscience-to-action gap. Close it.
