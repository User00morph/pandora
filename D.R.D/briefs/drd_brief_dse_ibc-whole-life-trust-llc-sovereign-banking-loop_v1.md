## THE FULL LOOP (operational summary)

```
LLC generates income (STIS + clients + ventures)
  → 95-98% K-1 → ILIT
  → ILIT funds policy premiums via split-dollar loan from LLC
  → Policy CSV compounds (4-6% + dividends, full CSV base — non-direct recognition)
  → ILIT requests policy loan from insurer (non-taxable)
  → ILIT loans proceeds to LLC via promissory note (AFR rate)
  → LLC deploys:
      STIS trading account → automated + discretionary positions
      Investment accounts → real estate, equities, crypto (self-custody)
      Business reserves → operational savings (LLC level)
  → STIS + investment returns flow back to LLC
  → LLC repays ILIT loan (interest = LLC expense deduction)
  → LLC generates K-1 → ILIT → cycle repeats
  → Policy CSV grows continuously through entire cycle
  → At death: policy death benefit flows to ILIT tax-free
                → perpetual trust for family / multi-generational transfer
```
