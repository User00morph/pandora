10 trading lessons that took me five years to learn for free in three minutes. Write this down, come back to this, save this, send this to a trading accountability partner. Number one, if you don't know the odds, you're gambling. Number two, if you let go of needing to win and make money every day, week or month, it's easy to make money every year. Number three, consistently profitable trading is an oxymoron. If you want to paycheck, go get a day job. Number four, the more perfectly optimized a system appears to be, the less likely it is to actually work in the future of live trading. Number five, if you can let go of being right and let go of needing to place the trades yourself, it becomes easy to make a lot of money. Number six, the more comfortable you are at stomaching a big drawdown, the bigger gains you can have when trading. Number seven, in trading you get paid to do things that are difficult, both emotionally and technically. Number eight, the less time you have for trading, the easier trading becomes. Number nine, learning the wrong thing is worse than learning nothing at all. Number ten, when you are ready, as a student, the teacher will appear.
