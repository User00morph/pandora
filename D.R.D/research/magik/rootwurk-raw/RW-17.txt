Something most people don't understand about magic is that magic isn't just about whether or not it works. It's also about how well it works, the quality of the unfolding of the outcome, how strongly and how cleanly it materializes. Most people have this binary one-dimensional black or white understanding of this craft. Did it work? Did it not work? It's the only question they know to ask, but there's levels to this shit. Yes, you can do powerful magic with no tools and no materials and it works. But does the work manifest faster with them? Yes, you can use a white candle for everything and it works. But does your candle magic hit different and manifest stronger with color magic? Yes, you can use plain olive oil for spiritual protection and it works. But does it work the same when you spend weeks virtually charging and consecrating that wall? Yes, you can use whatever you already have at home and it works. But does the magic manifest cleaner and sharper with no blowback when you're working with the best quality ingredients? Something working versus something being the best choice are two completely different things. A thing can function and still be the weakest version of what's available to you. And here's the part no one can do for you. The only way to know the difference for sure is to test it out for yourself in your own practice. Run the experiments, compare your outcomes, pay attention to what materializes and how it materializes. That's how you move from someone who just does magic to someone who's actually skilled at it. Whether it works is the floor, the bare minimum. How well it works is the mastery.
