### V4 — S-Corp Election Mechanics [NEW] (53s / 115w)
**Mechanism:**
1. Business receives EIN from IRS
2. Form multi-member LLC
3. File IRS Form 2553 → S-Corp election
4. File IRS Form 1120S (no federal income tax due at entity level)
5. Shareholders receive Schedule K-1 (ordinary business income + separately stated items)
6. K-1 income taxed as ordinary income on shareholder's personal return
7. Active shareholders must receive "reasonable compensation" via W-2

**D.R.D tier:** ESTABLISHED — this is standard IRS procedure, fully verifiable.
**Operational note:** The "reasonable compensation" requirement is a real IRS enforcement mechanism.
Active shareholders who skip W-2 payroll are audit targets (IRS Revenue Ruling 74-44).
This interacts with the zero-profit/trust architecture — W-2 must be structured carefully
relative to the K-1 distribution strategy.

**Cross-ref question:** If 95–98% goes to trust via K-1 and the shareholder also takes W-2,
does the W-2 come out of the 2–5% remainder, or from gross before the K-1 split?
This matters for the zero-profit model. Not addressed in source.
