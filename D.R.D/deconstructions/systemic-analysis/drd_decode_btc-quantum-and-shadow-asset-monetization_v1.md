**Original OS position (2026-06-11):** BTC quantum vulnerability is real but not imminent — ECDSA is the vulnerability vector, 2030-2033 horizon, monitor and reassess if upgrade progress stalls past 2028.

**New evidence — the timeline has moved closer, and the response has moved from theoretical to active engineering:**
- Google researchers now estimate a sufficiently powerful quantum computer could break Bitcoin's core cryptography in **under nine minutes**, with that capability potentially emerging **as soon as 2029** — a year earlier than the prior 2030-2033 estimate cited in the OS.
- The qubit requirement has dropped sharply: earlier models assumed millions of qubits to crack ECDSA-256; current research puts the figure at **~500,000 physical qubits** — meaning the threat surface is closer than previously modeled, not farther.
- Bitcoin developers have moved from discussion to formal proposal: **BIP-360** (removes on-chain public keys via a new Pay-to-Merkle-Root output type) and **BIP-361** ("Post Quantum Migration and Legacy Signature Sunset") are both live, named proposals — not speculative chatter.

Phase B (5 years post-activation) invalidates old ECDSA/Schnorr signatures entirely — coins left in old-style addresses become unspendable on the network. Phase C is a still-unproven zero-knowledge-proof rescue path for recovering frozen coins.
**Tier:** 1-2 (CoinDesk reporting on named, public BIP repository proposals; BIP text itself is Tier 1 primary source).

**Confidence revision:** POSSIBLE → **PROBABLE, with a materially tightened timeline.** The risk is no longer "a 2030-2033 horizon to monitor" — it is "a 2029 earliest-threat-emergence estimate with a formal, dated network migration plan already in motion." This is a genuine upgrade in urgency, not just confirmation.

**Direct action implication:** Any BTC held in old-style (pre-BIP-360) address formats faces a real, dated risk of forced freeze under BIP-361 Phase B if the holder does not migrate to quantum-resistant address types once activated. - Industry is in the "NISQ" era (Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum, 2024-2026): 100-2,000 qubit systems, limited error correction. IBM targets a "quantum advantage" demonstration in 2026 (Heron R2, 156-qubit processor); fault-tolerant modules targeted 2027. IonQ projects 2M+ physical qubits by 2030.
- **Real institutional capital is already deployed:** IonQ acquired chipmaker SkyWater Technology for $1.8B (Jan 2026) — first time a pure-play quantum company bought a chip fab outright, reducing external supplier dependence (vertical integration signal). PsiQuantum raised $2B+ privately at a $7B post-money valuation in a Series E **led by BlackRock** (Sept 2025).
- Honest limitation stated directly by industry sources: **there is still no commercial "killer app" running at scale on quantum hardware** in 2026. Near-term focus (2026-2028) is proving quantum advantage in narrow use cases (drug discovery, financial modeling, optimization) — not general commercial deployment.
**Tier:** 2-3 (trade press + named, verifiable funding rounds — BlackRock's participation is a strong institutional-conviction signal, traceable).

**Pattern:** BlackRock leading PsiQuantum's round is the single strongest signal here — the same institution at the center of the tokenization/RWA wave (per the trading-blockchain-batch2 reconstruction) is also positioning in quantum compute. This is consistent with a broader pattern already identified in the OS: BlackRock as the dominant operator migrating control functions onto every emerging infrastructure layer simultaneously (custody-controlled tokenization, AI/data-center infrastructure, now quantum).

- Market sizing: global water/wastewater sector projected to exceed $1 trillion by 2033 (infrastructure + rights + tech + utilities + finance combined).
**Tier:** 2-3 (industry/trade press, but underlying market mechanisms — NQH2O index, CME futures, Australian water rights trading — are Tier 1 verifiable financial infrastructure).

**Confidence:** PROBABLE. The "shadow monetization" framing in the prompt is accurate — water is moving toward becoming a tradable, financializable asset, but the actual market remains small, geographically narrow (3 regions with functioning rights markets), and tokenization is still speculative.
