## STAGE 4B — CONCEPT DISTILLATION

### CONCEPT: THE SPACE INFRASTRUCTURE THESIS

**Root:** AI needs compute. Compute needs infrastructure. Ground-based infrastructure has physical limits. Space removes those limits. The companies building the rails for space compute are positioned like early cloud infrastructure plays.

**Pillars:**
1. AI compute demand is structurally unbounded — growing faster than ground infrastructure can scale
2. Space removes the three ground constraints: land, power grid access, and cooling
3. Picks-and-shovels (Redwire, Rocket Labs) outperform the headline names (SpaceX) during build phases
4. Space infrastructure is defense-adjacent — US government (DoD, NASA) is a structural customer base independent of commercial demand
5. The SpaceX IPO creates a benchmark — all smaller space infrastructure names get re-rated upward when the sector gets a flagship public comp

**The picks-and-shovels principle applied to space:**
During the California Gold Rush, the men who sold picks and shovels (Levi Strauss) made more money than most miners. Applied here: SpaceX is the miner (flashy, narrative-heavy, capital-intensive). Redwire and Rocket Labs are the picks and shovels — they sell to SpaceX, to Boeing, to government — they make money regardless of who wins the space race.
