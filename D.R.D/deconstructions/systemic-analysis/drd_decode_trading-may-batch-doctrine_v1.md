## STREAM 1 — WEALTH ARCHITECTURE

### 1.1 Buy, Borrow, Die (Source: f7d8850678ba40b5)

High-net-worth strategy for growing wealth uninterrupted while legally minimizing taxes.

**The Doctrine:**
- **Buy** — Liquid A+ assets accepted as collateral (primary: S&P 500 ETF / SPY)
- **Borrow** — Take collateralized loans against the asset (up to 60% LTV at ~5% interest)
- **Die** — Never sell. Pass the asset stepped-up in basis. The loan never triggers a taxable event.

**The Math:**
- SPY averages ~10% annual growth
- Borrowing at 5% against it = net positive carry
- $1M SPY → borrow $600K → pay $30K/yr interest → SPY gains $100K → net gain: $70K + full $1M position intact
- Selling $600K instead: pay capital gains + lose compounding on that $600K forever

**The Application:**
- Use for liquidity events (real estate down payment, equipment, business investment) without triggering tax
- Only viable once you have a significant SPY position
- Short-term: build the SPY position. Medium-term: borrow against it. Long-term: never sell.

**Route:** → D.S.E/trading/skills/ as a wealth tier doctrine add-on to STIS

## STREAM 4 — TECHNICAL EXECUTION

### 4.1 All-Time Highs Entry Model (Source: 0e27635b3a6c46ff — May 28)
**Source:** William O'Neill / Dreyfus Fund analysis

**The Doctrine:**
- The best entry model is not: Fibonacci retracements, fair value gaps, discounts, supply zones
- The best entry model is: **Buy only new all-time highs**

**Why it works as both entry model AND screener:**
- Thousands of assets exist — buying discounts gives no filter for which discounts recover
- Many assets go to zero. Assets at ATH filter out everything heading to zero.
- ATH = only the strongest assets (those actually in uptrends) qualify
- It's long-only by design — you're always in the strongest names

**William O'Neill validation:** Nicholas Darvas, Mark Minervini, Oliver Kell, Stan Weinstein — all top stock championship winners use ATH-based entry models (different variations, same spine).

**STIS integration:** ATH filter is a screener layer — runs on top of IEC regime + GEX positioning to confirm entry alignment.

### 4.2 Order Flow Framework (Source: 5cfe95594889496f)

**The Framework: Context → Location → Confirmation**

1. **Context** — build the narrative before any trade
   - Observe breakouts at major structural points
   - Daily, weekly, overnight inventory levels
   - "What happened when price broke this level?"
   
2. **Location** — identify exact entry zone
   - Drill into what happened inside the breakout zone
   - Spot outliers: low volume nodes, delta spikes, volume spikes, POCs, big trades
   
3. **Confirmation** — validate before entry
   - **Absorption** = trapped sellers or buyers at your level
   - **Aggression** = volume imbalances + aggressive large trades
   - Both required to trigger entry

**STIS integration:** This is the tactical execution layer on top of IEC regime detection. IEC defines the bias; this framework defines the entry.
