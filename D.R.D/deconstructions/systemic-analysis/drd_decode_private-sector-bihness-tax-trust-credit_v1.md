### [VIDEO 3 — Never Show a Profit (3dcf8607)] | Tier 3
**Claim:** Smart business owners taxed only on net income — net zero = tax zero. LLC distributes 95–98% via K-1 → trust → whole life insurance. Individual takes 1099 remainder → donates to family foundation. Trust + foundation purchase whole life. Withdrawals as tax-free loans. Result: business, trust, and individual all show a loss.
**Tier note:** Tier 3 — short-form clip, no CPA/attorney cited, no specific code sections cited.
**Limitation:** The mechanics are directionally real. K-1 pass-through income to trusts = real. Whole life policy loans = non-taxable = real (IRC 7702). Family foundation deductibility = real. However: the educator omits the following real risk — the IRS "economic substance doctrine" (IRC 7701(o)) and "profit motive" rules (IRC 183, hobby loss rules) apply to businesses consistently showing zero profit. An LLC that never shows profit can be reclassified as not operating for profit, losing deduction eligibility. The zero-tax loop as described is achievable but requires ongoing compliance, CPA oversight, and business purpose documentation. It is NOT automatic or risk-free.
**Contradiction:** ⚠️ RISK GAP — educator presents this as universally safe. Reality: IRS audit trigger is elevated for perpetual zero-profit entities. Not a contradiction within this batch, but a significant omission vs. real-world operation.
