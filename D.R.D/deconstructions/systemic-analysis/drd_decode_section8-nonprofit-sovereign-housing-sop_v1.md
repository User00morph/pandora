## SECTION 1 — 18 USC §8: THE OBLIGATION DOCTRINE

### Legal Foundation (ESTABLISHED — statute cited directly)

**18 USC §8** — "Obligation or other security of the United States"

The argument: Section 8 is not welfare. It is the statutory fulfillment of the United States Corporation's mandatory obligation to the people of the land. The government created Federal Reserve Notes (debt instruments) to replace real money. In doing so, it assumed the obligation to satisfy those notes — including bills, checks, and all debt denominated in FRNs.

### The FRN / Money Distinction (ESTABLISHED — Black's Law cited)

> Black's Law 6: "Money — In usual and ordinary exception, it means coins and paper currency used as circulating mediums of exchange and does not embrace notes."

| Instrument | Classification | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Federal Reserve Note | Debt instrument (note) | You have not been paid — you hold their IOU |
| Gold / Silver | Money | Real value, real payment |
| Money Orders | Paper money (gold/silver backed) | Legitimate exchange medium |
| Stamps | Paper money (gold/silver backed) | Legitimate exchange medium |
| Paycheck | Check (note) | Not income — a transfer of their debt obligation |

**Core doctrine:** You cannot be taxed on income you have not received. FRNs are not income (return in money) because they are not money — they are notes. Therefore income tax on FRN wages = taxation on unreceived income.

### Section 8 as Treaty Obligation (ESTABLISHED — statutory basis)
- Government can only do business with a business (contract law)
- Section 8 is the mechanism by which the government satisfies its obligation to cover FRNs, bills, and notes
- All people of the land qualify — not just low income
- The "poor people" framing is a perception management operation to limit uptake and thereby limit government's total obligation exposure
- Prerequisite: a business entity must exist for the government to pay into — individual living persons are not a valid contracting party

## SECTION 2 — NON-PROFIT SOVEREIGN HOUSING SOP

### The Structure

**Entity:** Non-profit corporation (not LLC, not trust — specifically non-profit)

**Why non-profit:**
- You are a volunteer = you take no personal salary from the entity
- Zero personal income = qualifies for Section 8 (welfare/housing assistance)
- Non-profit can still hold assets, accumulate revenue, purchase property, own vehicles
- DUNS score (business credit) accrues to the non-profit — not your personal SSN

**Minimum viable structure:**
- 1 president/owner (you)
- 3-person board of directors (minimum): president + treasurer + secretary
- Board can be family members or trusted friends
- Assign: yourself as president, Dr. Nwosu / father / mother as board candidates
