Ẹdjọ (plural) are the spiritual forces/intelligences that operate between Ọghẹnẹ and the human realm. They are neither "gods" (colonial framing) nor "demons" (missionary framing). They are functional intelligences within a layered cosmological architecture.

2. **Clan/Community Ẹdjọ** — tutelary intelligences of specific places, clans, and waterways. The land/water has its own spiritual intelligence.

**Distortion applied by colonialism:** The Ẹdjọ were lumped together as "juju" and "idol worship" — erasing the precise cosmological taxonomy that distinguished personal, communal, and cosmic spiritual intelligence.
