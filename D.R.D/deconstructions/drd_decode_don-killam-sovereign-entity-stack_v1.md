## SECTION 3 — ACCOUNTING FRAMEWORK
**New to stack. Integrate.**

Killam's most technically grounded section. Directly verifiable against FASB/IRS sources.

### The Two Accounting Methods
| Method | Who Uses It | Status |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| Cash method (1040 filer) | Employees, consumers | Public sector — lowest position |
| Accrual method | Corporations, trusts | Private sector — revenue recorded when earned, not when paid |

**Form 3115** = IRS form to change accounting method from cash to accrual.

### GAAP Framework
- GAAP = Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (FASB — Financial Accounting Standards Board)
- **12 USC 18(2)** = all US banks mandated by statute to follow GAAP
- All US public companies must prepare certified financial statements under GAAP
- **Credit bureau reports are NOT certified financial statements** — they are not audited, not certified → this is the leverage point in disputes
- Certified financial statement = audited and signed by external accountant

### Key FASB Standards (relevant to securitization)
- **FAS 125** — Securitization accounting
- **FAS 140** — Offsetting financial assets and liabilities (connects to UCC 3-305 and 3-306)
- **FAS 133** — Derivatives on hedge accounts
- **FAS 5** — Accounting for contingencies
- **FAS 95** — Statement of cash flows
- Securitization: note (negotiable instrument under UCC Art. 3) → converted to security (UCC Art. 8) after securitization → legally this changes the nature of the obligation

### IRS Form Stack (accounting/dispute process)
| Form | Function |
|------|----------|
| 1040 | Personal income (cash method) — "gift to crown" in Killam's framing |
| 1041 | Trust/estate tax return — use this instead |
| 1040-V | Payment voucher |
| 1096 | Cover/transmittal for 1099 series |
| Form 56 | Appoints Secretary of Treasury as fiduciary (assessment agent) |
| Form 8281 | Identifies who you are to IRS |
| Form 3115 | Change accounting method |
| Form 3949-A | Information referral — triggers IRS audit on a third party |

## SECTION 4 — PRIVATE BANKING POSTURE
**Extends existing stack. Integrate.**

### Consumer vs Investor
- Consumer = public codes/statutes = lowest commercial status = Congress gives you nothing
- Investor = Congress protections active = SEC Investor Protection Act applies = more leverage
- Come in as private investor/private banker — not as consumer seeking remedy

### Admin Process (complaint ladder)
When challenging a creditor/debt/account:
1. File complaint directly with creditor (admin process, 10–21 days)
2. Send W9 requesting their EIN — they must certify response by affidavit; they won't
3. Form 3949-A → IRS (trigger audit on counterparty)
4. File investor complaint with SEC (as private investor)
5. SEC → opens MUI (Matter Under Inquiry) → tracks as case
6. FINRA arbitration (under FINRA arbitration clause in your credit agreements)
7. FTC complaint
8. State Attorney General (RICO if fraud is provable)
9. Federal district court (sue the state court for fraud if needed)

### Securitization Audit
- Request a securitization audit / Bloomberg report on your debt
- Proves whether your note was securitized (converted from negotiable instrument to security)
- Once securitized, original lender no longer holds the note — they cannot prove standing
- This is Jean Keaton's model (RICO suits against banks — mentioned as reference)
- **Must have audit proof before going to higher courts or AG**

### SEC Filing Templates (Killam's private school)
- SEC investor complaint (as private citizen/sole private investor)
- FINRA investor complaint
- FTC complaint
- Total: 10 agency templates in Killam's system

### Amicus Curiae (Marcus Curiae)
- Non-party who appears in court to provide information
- Use when appearing on behalf of the family/trust
- Natural person can appear sui juris
- Attorneys = bar membership only (not license) — "practice of law is an occupation of common right"

## SECTION 9 — CONTRADICTIONS / FLAGS

**FLAG 1 — Killam presents 1099-OID as straightforward. Stream B says federal jail risk.**
Resolution: Stream B governs. Killam even confirms the risk (sovereign citizen label raised his legal costs). HELD.

**FLAG 2 — Killam says "anyone can practice law, no license required."**
Reality: partially true (common right to represent yourself). You cannot represent others in court without bar membership in most jurisdictions. Sui juris = represent yourself only. Amicus Curiae = non-party information only.

**FLAG 3 — Killam's BAR = British Accreditation Regency.**
Reality: Google searches show this is disputed. The more accurate etymology is that "bar" refers to the physical railing in a courtroom. The "British Accreditation Regency" acronym appears to be a folk etymology. Does not change the substantive point that bar membership is a private association, not a government license.

**FLAG 4 — Killam's W4 Sandwich method.**
Killam himself admits: experimental, mixed results. Do not implement without more vetting. Not added to stack.

**FLAG 5 — Manufacturing spend / invoice creation.**
Legal if invoices represent legitimate internal transactions. Illegal (bank fraud) if fabricated. Must stay within real business service flows between entities.
