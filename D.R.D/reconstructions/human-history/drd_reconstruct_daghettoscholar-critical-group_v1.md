### 1. WHAT IS NOW KNOWN WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE

**F1 — Pre-Clovis indigenous human presence in North America is confirmed at Tier 1.**
The Page-Ladson site (near Tallahassee, Florida) contains stone tools and mastodon bone cut-marks dated to ~14,500 years ago — approximately 1,500 years before the Clovis culture previously considered the earliest in the Americas. This is a peer-reviewed archaeological site (Halligan et al. 2016). The "Clovis First" model is no longer the scientific consensus.

**F2 — The oldest known human Y-chromosome haplogroup (A00) originates in a man born in Florida before US territorial acquisition.**
Albert Perry (born ~1816-1819, Florida) carries haplogroup A00, published by Mendez et al. (2013) in the American Journal of Human Genetics. His SNP profile does not match the Cameroonian A00a lineage — the genetic signature is distinct and was present in Florida at a time when Florida was not under US jurisdiction. The confluence of Page-Ladson (place) + Perry (lineage) constitutes convergent Tier 1 evidence for a deep indigenous presence in the Florida/southeastern region specifically.

**F3 — DNA ancestry test reference panels were constructed without North American indigenous samples, using a methodology that structurally erases Black indigenous ancestry.**
The 1000 Genomes Project built its "Native American" reference by: (a) using admixed Central/South American populations as proxies, (b) applying chromosomal masking to strip African and European genetic segments, (c) retaining East Asian-origin markers as the indigenous baseline. This operationalizes the Bering Strait single-migration theory at the algorithmic level. Any genetic marker identified as "indigenous" by these tests is, by construction, East Asian-proximate. Any Black American whose ancestors were North American indigenous receives an "African" result because the African-origin segments in the reference population were removed before the baseline was defined.

**F4 — There is a structural double standard in how admixture is treated across racial identity categories.**
Modern federally recognized indigenous people's DNA tests strip their African/European admixture before assigning the indigenous label. Black Americans' tests do not strip admixture — it is displayed. The same ancestry company, the same algorithm, two asymmetric applications. The asymmetry is documented in the methodology, not asserted — it is a mathematical consequence of how the reference panel was built.

**F5 — Cross-cultural contact between Aboriginal Americans and Africans pre-dates Columbus.**
Confirmed across multiple independent sources already in the OS: cocaine in Egyptian mummies (ESTABLISHED, Group D), plant trade evidence, DaGhettoScholar's cranial deformation parallels (Americas + Africa, independently practiced), and Columbus journal references to Mayan kings. **F6 — The cartographic evidence used to argue "America = TaMeri (Egypt)" does not hold.**
The primary map cited in TaMeri arguments is Dutch (Peter Van Der Eye, 1706 mapmaker, known pirate-printer of foreign editions). The Dutch phrase on the map does not mean TaMeri — it refers to a Dutch-language designation for Florida.
