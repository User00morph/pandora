## 1. WHAT IS NOW KNOWN WITH HIGH CONFIDENCE

This is not one guru's system — it is a convention independently taught by multiple unaffiliated sources, which is meaningful convergence even though none of them offer audited results. The D.TRD framework's Layer 1 ("no intraday entry without HTF alignment... a setup without HTF confirmation is not a setup") is not a novel invention; it is a documented, widely-practiced discipline.

**Demand/supply zone identification (the last opposing candle before the impulse move) and liquidity-sweep-as-entry-trigger are both corroborated, and both are already present in the framework's Layer 2 and Layer 5.** The playlist adds no new mechanism here — it confirms the existing one from independent angles. Notably, one mentor (video 8) explicitly separates imbalance/FVG-as-target from imbalance-as-entry ("I don't trade imbalances... I'm going to set my limit at the top of this and if it comes to get me it comes to get me") — this is the same target/entry separation the framework already encodes in Layer 6 ("volume gap zones... targets and stop-placement guardrails, not entries").

**Marketing-tier win-rate claims in this batch (80–90%) are unverifiable and must not be read as evidence that the framework's real 35.7% win rate is "underperforming" a comparable system.** Three sources in this playlist (videos 5, 14, 16) claim 80–90% win rates on methodologically similar setups (harmonic patterns, EMA retracement, EMA+Fibonacci). All three drive toward a paid product (broker referral, Telegram mentorship). The D.TRD framework's 35.7% win rate / 0.66 profit factor is the one number in this entire body of material — across both this playlist and the framework itself — that is backed by an actual, itemized, dated trade log. Unaudited marketing claims of 80–90% carry no evidentiary weight against that; they are Tier 4–5 by design and were never eligible to override it.
