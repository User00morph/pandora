## SOVEREIGN POSITION STATEMENTS

**SP-1:** A participating whole life insurance policy from a non-direct recognition mutual company, properly designed with a PUA rider and MEC-compliant structure, is the most complete sovereign capital instrument available within the existing financial system. It is simultaneously a savings account, a private lending institution, an estate transfer mechanism, and a tax shelter — with all three tax advantages converging in one instrument no other financial product matches.

**SP-2:** The Irrevocable Life Insurance Trust (ILIT) is the correct ownership vehicle. The policy must be owned by the trust from issuance — not transferred after the fact. Trust jurisdiction: Nevada or Wyoming (sovereign-aligned, no state income tax, strongest asset protection statutes). The trust removes the policy from personal estate, eliminates estate taxation on the death benefit, and wraps the asset in spendthrift creditor protection.

**SP-3:** Policy loans are not taxable events and carry no repayment schedule. This is the operational core of the sovereign banking function — the ability to access capital without triggering income recognition. All deployed capital (STIS trading, investments, reserves) flows via this loan mechanism, never via withdrawal.

**SP-4:** The LLC is the borrower and capital deployment vehicle. All trading accounts, investment positions, and business reserves are held at the LLC level. The trust loans policy proceeds to the LLC via a properly documented promissory note at or above the applicable AFR. The LLC's returns repay the loan and generate K-1 distributions back to the trust, which funds future premiums — closing the loop.

**SP-5:** The split-dollar arrangement (loan regime per IRS Notice 2002-8) allows the LLC to fund policy premiums, compressing the cash value build timeline. This is the gate that unlocks full early velocity. Requires CPA + attorney to structure. Cannot be DIY. This is not a shortcut — it is a precision instrument.

**SP-6:** Non-direct recognition is non-negotiable as a company selection criterion. Confirm in writing before application that the policy will not reduce the dividend credit on the loaned portion of CSV. If the agent cannot confirm this in writing, choose a different company.

**SP-7:** The timing architecture governs deployment strategy. Years 1-3: policy builds, no heavy borrowing. Years 3-7: light deployment (under 50% LTV). Year 7+: full deployment capacity. Starting the policy today matters more than any other variable. Each year of delay is a year of compounding permanently lost.

## POLICY DESIGN SPECIFICATION

When working with the insurance agent, provide this specification:

```
POLICY DESIGN REQUIREMENTS:
- Type: Participating whole life (not universal, not variable, not term)
- Company: Non-direct recognition mutual carrier
- Structure: Maximum CSV accumulation design
- Base premium: Minimum allowed
- PUA rider: Maximum PUA loading (target 60-75% of total premium as PUAs)
- MEC compliance: Policy must NOT become a Modified Endowment Contract
- Test: CVAT (Cash Value Accumulation Test) preferred
- Death benefit: Accept lower face amount in exchange for higher CSV ratio
- Illustration requested: Year 1, 5, 10, 20 CSV vs. death benefit vs. dividend projections
- Confirm in writing: Non-direct recognition (dividend not reduced on borrowed CSV)

RED FLAGS IN THE ILLUSTRATION:
- High face amount relative to premium → wrong design
- Year 5 CSV significantly below total premiums paid → poorly designed
- Agent cannot confirm non-direct recognition in writing → wrong carrier
- No PUA rider offered → wrong agent
```

## ARM'S-LENGTH LOAN PROTOCOL (ILIT → LLC)

Every time the ILIT makes a loan to the LLC:

```
STEP 1 — Determine loan amount
  Request policy loan from insurer (trust as policy owner)
  Insurer wires to ILIT bank account

STEP 2 — Draft promissory note
  Parties: ILIT (lender) + LLC (borrower)
  Principal: loan amount
  Interest rate: at or above current AFR (short/mid/long-term per term length)
  Repayment: defined schedule (annual minimum, flexible payoff allowed)
  Purpose: business investment/operations (state the specific use)
  Signed: Morph as trustee (ILIT) + Morph as LLC manager/member

STEP 3 — Wire proceeds to LLC
  From ILIT account to LLC business checking
  Record: inter-entity transfer per promissory note dated [date]

STEP 4 — Track and repay
  LLC books the loan as a liability
  Interest accrues and is paid annually (deductible LLC expense)
  Principal repayments documented
  CPA maintains records

STEP 5 — Policy loan interest
  LLC pays ILIT loan interest → ILIT uses it to pay policy loan interest to insurer
  The spread (if any between AFR and policy loan rate) is managed by CPA
```
