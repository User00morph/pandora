## 5. TRX — SUSTAINABILITY UNDER TETHER REGULATORY PRESSURE

**Verified, mixed result:**
- **Risk confirmed as real and active, not hypothetical:** Tether froze $182M USDT on Tron in January 2026 and $344M in April 2026, both tied to law-enforcement coordination on illicit activity. This shows USDT-on-Tron is *not* outside US enforcement reach in practice — undercutting the "raising enforcement concerns" framing that assumed regulators couldn't touch it.
- **Tether is actively building its own settlement layer** to reduce reliance on Tron — confirming the original risk (TRX's core utility case depends on a relationship Tether itself may be moving away from). Migration is described as likely slow, given Tron's deep liquidity and low fees.
- **Legal overhang reduced, not increased:** On March 5, 2026, the SEC dismissed all claims against the TRON Foundation — removing a significant legal risk and clearing space for broader institutional engagement, which cuts against the "regulated off Tron" downside scenario.
- Tron still carries ~45% of total USDT supply (~$89B+) and remains the dominant chain for USDT payments/remittances, especially in Southeast Asia and Latin America — the operational utility described in the original reconstruction is current and intact.

**Confidence revision:** NEEDS EVIDENCE → **PROBABLE (uncertainty narrows, doesn't resolve).** Net effect of this verification pass: the *legal* risk to TRX is lower than assumed (SEC dismissal), but the *structural* risk is more concrete than assumed (Tether is actually building an alternative rail, not just facing pressure passively). The original PROBABLE rating on "TRX fundamentally undervalued" holds; the specific risk identified in the open question (regulatory removal) is now better understood as a longer, slower-moving threat than a sudden one.
