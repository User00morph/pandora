## PREPARATION METHOD

From the source's personal protocol:
1. Combine all ingredients — start with recommended quantities (do not scale up)
2. Concentrate into a **small container** — do not dilute into a large batch
3. Allow to set/harden (coconut oil solidifies at room temperature)
4. **Take 1 tablespoon daily** — swallow the concentrated paste
5. Source notes: larger, diluted quantities worked less well — concentration matters

**Why concentration matters:** Curcumin (turmeric's active compound) is notoriously low-bioavailability when dispersed. Concentration + fat carrier (coconut oil) + small dose appears to optimize absorption. This is consistent with herb pharmacology: many adaptogens and anti-inflammatories have dose-response curves where concentrated small doses outperform diluted large ones.
