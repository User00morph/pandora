## SECTION 7 — INTERNAL TRIBUNAL

**7.1 Purpose:** Resolve all disputes among Ministry members privately, without recourse to external courts.

**7.2 Composition:** High Priest (presiding) + 2 Elders.

**7.3 Jurisdiction:** All disputes among members arising from Ministry affairs, private contracts, or Ministry instruments.

**7.4 Process:**
1. Written complaint filed with High Priest
2. Respondent has 7 days to respond in writing
3. Tribunal convenes (in person or remotely) within 14 days
4. Each party presents their case — no external attorneys required
5. Tribunal deliberates privately
6. Written decision issued within 7 days of hearing
7. Decision is final and binding among members

**7.5 Exhaustion requirement:** No member may bring a Ministry matter to any external court without first exhausting the Tribunal process. Breach of this provision = grounds for membership revocation.

**7.6 External enforcement:** Tribunal decisions may be enforced as private arbitration awards in commercial courts if the external party has signed a Ministry contract containing an arbitration clause.

## SECTION 12 — DISSOLUTION

**12.1** The House of Auset may be dissolved only by unanimous written consent of all founding officers, or by the High Priest alone if no other founding officers are living.

**12.2** Upon dissolution, all Ministry assets transfer to a designated successor private trust or Ministry, as directed by the High Priest.

**12.3** No Ministry assets shall transfer to any government entity upon dissolution.
