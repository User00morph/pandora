## DOCUMENT NUMBERING SYSTEM

All House of Auset instruments use this format:

```
HOA-[TYPE]-[YYYY]-[####]

TYPE CODES:
  BC  = Baptismal Certificate
  PN  = Patent of Nativity
  LS  = Letter of Standing
  ORD = Ordination / Ministerial Credential
  PC  = Private Contract
  TR  = Tribunal Decision
  AM  = Amendment to Bylaws

EXAMPLE: HOA-BC-2026-0001
```
