# DOM_SOP — Working Altar Setup v1
## The Spell-Work Station — Materials, Layout, First Workings

---

## STRUCTURAL LAW — TWO ALTARS, TWO SURFACES

**The working altar and the ancestral altar are separate stations. Non-negotiable.**

The Niger Delta Altar Protocol is explicit: the ancestral surface belongs to that
channel only — no unrelated objects, no spell work. The ancestral altar is a
communication technology. The working altar is a manifestation workshop.

```
ANCESTRAL ALTAR                      WORKING ALTAR
────────────────                     ─────────────
Communication channel                Spell-craft station
White cloth, white candles only      Full candle spectrum incl. black
Offerings + libations                Jars, roots, petitions, workings
Devotion practice                    Active castings
Woyingi / Erherhe / Sekiapu          Money, protection, banishing,
                                     sweetening, reversal work
```

They can share a room. They do not share a surface.

## PART I — THE FOUR ELEMENTS BASE

Every working needs an element match: *Am I rushing it (fire), healing it (water),
sending it (air), or making it last (earth)?* The altar keeps all four active.

| Element | On the altar | Function |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| **EARTH** | Dish of soil or salt · stones · living plants · bank dirt (once collected) | Anchor. Holds, protects, makes work last |
| **WATER** | Working bowl/vessel — separate from the ancestral bowl | Conductor. Carries, cleanses, washes |
| **FIRE** | Candles (Part II) | Catalyst. Forces movement, destroys delays |
| **AIR** | Incense — sage, frankincense | Elevator. Carries intention between realms |

## PART II — CANDLE TOOLKIT

Tea lights suffice for most work. Spell candles for longer workings.

| Color | Stock | Use |
|-------|-------|-----|
| **White** | Plenty | Generalist — blessing, cleansing, sweetening |
| **Black** | Plenty | The specialist — banishing, uncrossing, reversal, binding, cord cutting, shadow work. Layer 15: "not optional — optimal" |
| **Orange / yellow** | A few | Road opener |
| **Pink** | A few | Sweetening |

**Supporting tools:**
- Carving tool — nail, pin, or knife (road opener/blockbuster require carved candles)
- Anointing oil — any spell oil or clean carrier oil spoken over
- Fireproof dish / candle holders

## PART VIII — FIRST WORKINGS SEQUENCE

**Do not open this altar with a money spell.** The Sequencing Law (Layer 1)
governs: casting into a blocked field produces minimal results.

```
SESSION 1 — CLEAR
  1. Banish the space (black candle + intention — Layer 14: banish BEFORE cleanse)
  2. Cleanse (sage / salt / Psalms 91)
  3. Ground (bare feet, solid food, cold water)
  4. Protect (seal the space — spoken declaration of access)

SESSION 2 — OPEN
  5. Road opener candle — orange/yellow, carve "OPEN MY ROADS" bottom-up,
     anoint, dress with sugar + cinnamon + coffee
  6. Blockbuster candle — black or white, carve "CLEAR MY PATHS /
     REMOVE NEGATIVE BLOCKAGES", same dressing, same session or back-to-back
  → Expect movement in 5–7 days

PARALLEL — DAILY (belief work, Layer 1 + 21)
  7. Affirmation sequence: general ("I am safe. I am worthy.") → directional →
     specific. Do not cast targeted work until Phase 3 lands in the body.

THEN — CAST
  8. Money work sequence (Layer 7), protection installs (Layer 2 — name ward
     + cloak), or whatever the season calls for.

AFTER EVERY HEAVY WORKING
  9. Sweeten (Layer 13) — honey, chamomile, milk + bread. Not weekly. After
     every heavy working.
```

## OPERATING LAWS AT THIS ALTAR

1. **Sequence first** — stabilize → prioritize → open roads → bust blocks → cast
2. **Belief is the spell** — sit with the outcome until entitled to it; then cast
3. **Speak as done** — "I have," never "I want"
4. **Simplicity amplifies** — fewer variables, less doubt
5. **Hidden magician** — announce nothing, to anyone
6. **Active participation** — jars get shaken, bowls get fed; spells are collaborations
7. **Banish before cleanse** — or the foreign energy stays under the fresh surface
8. **Sweeten after heavy work** — or the dense field repels abundance too
9. **Timing when useful** — Thursday money, Saturday heavy, Tuesday multi-purpose,
   Sunday no casting (Layer 5); drop timing if it becomes pressure (P7)
