## WHAT THIS REQUEST IS FOR

**PIQ-99-05** is a Policy Interpretation Quarterly (PIQ) issued by the Social Security Administration in 1999. Stream B sources identify it as the document that addresses reconveyance vs. abandonment of the SS card and confirms that SSN disclosure is voluntary in most contexts.

This FOIA request seeks the full text of that document to:
- Confirm its exact scope, language, and operative status
- Establish whether it is still in effect or has been superseded
- Obtain the primary source for any reconveyance procedure it describes
- Build out the foundational documentation stack for Layer 2 (jurisdictional identity)

**Status of the document:** Referenced across Stream B research — citation needs verification. This request obtains the source directly from SSA rather than relying on secondary citations.

**Risk profile:** Standard FOIA request — fully legal, no exposure. SSA must respond within 20 business days.

## AFTER YOU SEND IT

**Timeline:** SSA must acknowledge within 10 business days and substantively respond within 20 business days. If no response, follow up in writing citing 5 U.S.C. § 552(a)(6)(A).

**If they say the document doesn't exist:**
This is itself intelligence — either (a) the document was misfiled under a different designation, (b) it was rescinded without notice, or (c) Stream B's citation is inaccurate. Request a search certification confirming no responsive records exist. Then run a secondary FOIA to the SSA Office of the Inspector General for any record of that PIQ number.

**If they say it's exempt:**
Note the specific exemption claimed. FOIA exemptions for internal policy documents are narrow — most PIQ documents are administrative guidance, not law enforcement material. An exemption claim here is worth appealing.

**What to do with the document when received:**
1. File original at `D.R.D/research/human-history/` as a primary source document
2. Run through D.R.D Stage 3 — compare SSA's own language against Stream B's characterization

4. If it does not: update the reconstruction file's confidence score accordingly — Stream B's reconveyance claim drops from PROBABLE
