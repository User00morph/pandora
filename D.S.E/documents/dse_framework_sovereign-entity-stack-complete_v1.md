## THE FUNDING CYCLE

```
LLC earns active income (consulting, dev, trading)
    ↓ distributes profits to Member (Trust or Ministry)
Trust / Ministry receives and accumulates
    ↓ Trust loans to beneficiary at AFR (IRS §1274(d))
Beneficiary receives loan proceeds — NOT taxable income
    ↓ builds, invests, deploys capital
    ↓ repays trust over time with interest
Trust grows — cycle repeats
```

**Key:** Loan proceeds ≠ income. No tax event on receipt. AFR interest flows back into trust. Trust accumulates. Beneficiary gets capital without triggering kiddie tax or income tax.
