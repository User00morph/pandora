## WHAT THIS REQUEST IS FOR

The NUMIDENT (Numerical Identification System) is the SSA's master database record for each SSN. It contains:
- The original **SS-5 application** — the form your parents signed when they applied for your SSN, which Stream B identifies as the founding contract document
- Your full SSN account record as the SSA holds it
- Account type, status, and all associated data fields
- Any linked record systems

Under the Privacy Act of 1974, you have the right to access your own records held by any federal agency. This is a clean, low-risk, fully legal records request — no legal exposure.

**What we expect to learn:**
- Exact terms of the original SS-5 filing (who filed it, what was attested to, under what authority)
- Full account structure as SSA recognizes it
- Any language confirming card/account terms not visible on the card face

**What may be withheld:**
- Internal financial linkages to Treasury (those records sit under Treasury/IRS jurisdiction, not SSA — separate request if needed)
- Any fields exempted under Privacy Act subsection (j) or (k)

## AFTER YOU SEND IT

**Timeline:** SSA must acknowledge within 10 business days and respond within 20 business days. If they don't, follow up in writing citing 5 U.S.C. § 552a(d)(1).

**What to do with the response:**
1. File the original response in `D.R.D/research/human-history/` as a raw source document
2. Run it through D.R.D Stage 3 — compare what SSA says the account is against Stream B's interpretation
3. Note any fields, codes, or designations that don't match standard identity records
4. This becomes the primary source material for the jurisdictional identity layer (Layer 2 of the sovereign architecture)

**If they deny the request:**
You have the right to appeal the denial to SSA's Appeals Officer, then to federal district court. Denials must cite a specific Privacy Act exemption — note exactly which one they cite, as that itself is intelligence about what they're holding.

**Parallel request to consider (after this one):**
Once the NUMIDENT is in hand, a separate Privacy Act request to the **Treasury Department** (for any linked trust/account records) and a **FOIA request to the SSA for PIQ-99-05** (the Policy Interpretation Quarterly document Stream B cites confirming SSN disclosure is voluntary) would complete the documentation stack.
