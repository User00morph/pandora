## THE DOCTRINE

The sovereign entity stack does not minimize taxes. It reroutes income through instruments where taxation structurally does not apply. Every dollar entering this architecture either disappears as a legitimate expense, converts to K-1 income in a tax-sheltered trust, funds a policy where it grows tax-deferred, or gets borrowed back out as non-taxable debt.

The goal is not a lower rate. The goal is near-zero taxable income — by design, not by accident.

## FULL TARGET ARCHITECTURE

```
MORPH (natural person / ordained minister)
  ↓ controls all layers — owns none

KEMETIC PRIVATE MINISTRY TRUST
  ← Common law, unincorporated — no state filing required
  ← Automatically tax-exempt (church/ministry status)
  ← Receives services from Auset Solutions at cost
  ← Family volunteers receive all ministry services
  ← Permanent expense sink — LLC shows no taxable profit
  ← Files: nothing (unincorporated, exempt)

AUSET SOLUTIONS LLC → S-Corp (Form 2553, Phase 3 gate)
  ← NM filing, EIN, ausetsolutions.com
  ← Revenue: agentic engineering, ICM workflow builds,
     dev software, automation cultivation, vibe coding services
  ← Accounting method: ACCRUAL (Form 3115, file at Phase 3)
  ← NAICS: 541511 (Custom Computer Programming Services)
  ← Manufacturing spend SOP active (compresses taxable income)
  ← W-2 to Morph (reasonable comp — $40-60K range, Phase 3+)
  ← K-1 → 95-98% → Nevada ILIT
  ← 1099 → 2-5% → Kemetic Ministry Trust

NEVADA ILIT (Irrevocable Life Insurance Trust)
  ← Non-grantor trust = separate taxpayer (Phase 3)
  ← Receives K-1 from Auset Solutions
  ← Files: Form 1041 (trust income tax return)
  ← Owns: whole life policy (CSV compounds inside trust)
  ← Owns: Wyoming Trading LLC membership interest
  ← Policy loans (non-taxable) → back to Auset for hardware/ops
  ← Crummey provisions required for premium gift tax exclusion

WYOMING TRADING LLC
  ← Single-member LLC, NOT S-Corp (disregarded or partnership)
  ← Owned by ILIT (not by Morph personally)
  ← Active: STIS automation system (options + wheel + GEX)
  ← Long-term holds: BTC, LINK, RKLB, IREN, ASTS, PL
  ← §475 trader tax status election (ordinary losses, not capital)
  ← Liability wall — trading losses cannot reach Auset Solutions

WHOLE LIFE POLICY (inside ILIT)
  ← CSV grows tax-deferred (§7702)
  ← Policy loans: non-taxable (§72e)
  ← Death benefit: income-tax free to ILIT (§101a)
  ← Non-direct recognition carrier required (Guardian, Penn Mutual,
     MassMutual, Ohio National — confirmed historically)
  ← PUA rider: maximizes CSV without triggering MEC
  ← Funds: Node 3 hardware (Mac Mini M4 Pro), Auset operations
```

**HoldCo note:** Not in this architecture. The ILIT owns the Trading LLC. That IS the holding function. A Wyoming HoldCo becomes relevant only when a third operating entity spins up. Build it then, not now.

Personal credit lives on SSN — that doesn't change. Business credit is a parallel track built on EIN + D-U-N-S. The goal: LLC qualifies for credit on its own so SSN never enters a business transaction again. Timeline: 6-12 months of intentional tradeline building from LLC formation.

**D-U-N-S Number**
- Register at dnb.com — free (30-day processing) or $299 expedited
- This is the LLC's business credit ID — required for all D&B reporting
- Get this Day 1. The 30-day clock starts here.

**Business Profile Setup — must match across all applications**
```
Legal name:   AUSET SOLUTIONS LLC (exact as filed)
EIN:          [EIN from SS-4]
Address:      Registered agent address (Bizee) or virtual mailbox
Phone:        VoIP business line
Email:        [name]@ausetsolutions.com
NAICS:        541511 (Custom Computer Programming Services)
```
Consistency is non-negotiable. Mismatched name/address = declined applications.
