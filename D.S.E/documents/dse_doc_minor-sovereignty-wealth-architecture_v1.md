## CORE PRINCIPLE

A minor cannot sign contracts, form an LLC, or hold a standard EIN as a principal. But a **trust can be established FOR them** — naming them as beneficiary — with a guardian/trustee controlling the entity. That trust gets its own EIN. Commerce, assets, and wealth flow through the trust. The minor is the beneficiary. When they turn 18, they can take over the trust, form their own LLC, and transfer everything.

This is not a workaround. This is the same structure used by generational wealth families from the moment a child is born.

---

## WHY LOANS ARE THE MECHANISM

**Loan proceeds are not taxable income.** This is the leverage point.

When a trust distributes cash to a beneficiary, that distribution may be taxable (especially under the "Kiddie Tax" — IRC §1(g) — which taxes unearned income above $2,500 at the parent's marginal rate for children under 19). But when a trust **loans** money to a beneficiary, the proceeds are a liability — not income. No tax event.

**Requirements for the loan to hold:**
- Must be documented with a signed promissory note
- Must charge at least the IRS Applicable Federal Rate (AFR — published monthly, currently ~4-5%)
- Must have a repayment schedule
- Without these, IRS recharacterizes the loan as a taxable distribution

The trust charges AFR interest → the interest flows back into the trust → the trust accumulates. The beneficiary receives loan proceeds tax-free → uses capital to build, invest, or acquire assets.

---

## THE KIDDIE TAX — WHAT IT IS + HOW THE STRUCTURE NAVIGATES IT

IRC §1(g): Unearned income (dividends, interest, capital gains) above $2,500 per year earned by a child under 19 is taxed at the **parent's** marginal rate, not the child's rate.

This means simply dumping investment accounts into a minor's name doesn't create tax savings — the income is still taxed at the parent's rate.

**How the trust structure navigates this:**

| Method | Tax Result |
|--------|-----------|
| Direct distribution from trust to minor | Kiddie tax applies — taxed at parent's rate |
| Trust retains/accumulates income inside trust | Taxed at trust rates (separate from parent) |
| Trust loans proceeds to minor | NOT a distribution — not taxable income at all |
| Minor earns wages from real work | Ordinary income, but below standard deduction (~$15K) = zero federal tax |

**Strategy:** Trust accumulates inside the trust (no kiddie tax on accumulated trust income — it stays in the trust's tax bracket). Trust makes loans to beneficiary when capital is needed. Distributions timed to after age 19 when kiddie tax no longer applies.

## STRUCTURE BY AGE

### Age 10 — Foundation Build

**What they can't do:**
- Sign contracts
- Form LLC
- Open bank accounts independently

**What can be established for them:**

| Action | Who Does It | Purpose |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| Non-grantor irrevocable trust | Guardian/trustee forms, minor is beneficiary | Entity with own EIN, holds assets |
| EIN via IRS SS-4 | Trustee applies, trust is the entity | Commerce, investing, account opening |
| ARNA registration | Parent/guardian registers minor | Sovereignty foundation — body politic |
| Baptismal certificate | ULC-ordained family member issues | Private birth record — status correction foundation |
| Baby land deed / patent of nativity | Filed into private record | Private land jurisdiction documentation |
| IBC whole life policy | Parent/trustee opens, minor is insured | Cheapest premiums possible — CSV builds for decades |

**IBC note:** A whole life policy opened on a 10-year-old is some of the cheapest insurance available. The cash surrender value compounds for 8+ years before any of the other structure is fully deployed. By the time they're 18, there's a meaningful CSV base they can borrow against tax-free. Open this now — every year of delay is lost compounding.

**Trust investment pathway:**
- Trust EIN → open investment account (custodial brokerage under trust)
- Invest into assets inside the trust
- Income accumulates at trust tax rates (not kiddie tax)
- When minor needs capital: trust loans at AFR — not taxable

### Age 16 — Activation Layer

**What they can do:**
- Work and earn real income
- Have a driver's license
- Participate in the trust structure (understand it, begin learning)

**Additional structures available:**

| Action | Legal Basis | Benefit |
|--------|------------|---------|
| Earn wages from family entity for real work | IRC §73 — child's income is child's income | Up to standard deduction (~$15K) = zero federal income tax |
| Trust holds LLC → LLC earns income | EIN separate from minor's SSN | Business activity under entity — not personal income |
| Vehicle titled to family trust/LLC | Trust as legal owner | Removes personal liability exposure |
| Authorized user on family credit | No age requirement | Begins building credit history immediately |
| ARNA private conveyance documentation | ARNA body politic | Layer 5 of sovereign safeguard architecture — builds on existing identity foundation |

**On wages:** The work must be real. Family can pay a 16-year-old for legitimate services — social media management, admin work, cleaning, delivery. Documented. Paid through the entity. Up to ~$15K/year effectively tax-free because it's below the standard deduction threshold. No FICA if paid from a sole proprietorship or single-member LLC taxed as sole prop (IRC §3121(b)(3)(A) — applies when parent employs their own child under 18).

*Note: If the payer entity is not the minor's parent — e.g., a sibling's LLC — FICA applies, but the income is still below the standard deduction for federal income tax purposes.*

## THE EIN STRATEGY — ONE PER TRUST

Each trust is a separate legal entity with its own EIN. Multiple family members = multiple trusts = multiple EINs. This is legitimate estate and family wealth architecture.

```
Guardian/Trustee
    │
    ├── Trust A (EIN: XXX-XX-XXXX) → Beneficiary: Minor Age 10
    │       └── Investment account, IBC policy, assets
    │
    ├── Trust B (EIN: XXX-XX-XXXX) → Beneficiary: Minor Age 16
    │       └── Investment account, LLC membership, vehicle title
    │
    └── Trust C (EIN: XXX-XX-XXXX) → Beneficiary: Minor Age 17
            └── Investment account, pre-launch LLC structure
```

Each trust:
- Has its own EIN (not tied to the minor's SSN)
- Files its own Form 1041 (trust tax return)
- Can hold bank accounts, brokerage accounts, LLCs, real estate, insurance policies
- Makes loans to beneficiaries at AFR — proceeds are non-taxable

## PRIVATE NATIONAL + IDENTITY CORRECTION — BUILD EARLY

The status correction affidavit (declaring natural person status) is filed at 18. But the **foundational documents** can and should be established before 18 — they make the status correction stronger and more complete when filed.

| Document | When to Establish | Purpose |
|----------|-----------------|---------|
| Certified birth certificate | Now — request from vital records | Foundation document for everything |
| Baptismal certificate (private) | Now — ULC-ordained family member issues | Parallel private birth record |
| ARNA registration | Now — parent/guardian files on behalf of minor | Body politic membership — nationality foundation |
| Baby land deed / patent of nativity | Now | Private land jurisdiction claim |
| Status correction affidavit | At 18 — minor files themselves | Formal declaration: natural person, not legal person |
| Non-taxpayer statement | At 18 | Works in tandem with status correction |
| ARNA nationality card | At 18 (or earlier if ARNA allows through parent registration) | Primary private national ID for encounters |

**Key:** The private record-building is not waiting for anything. It begins the moment you decide to begin it. The affidavit files at 18 but it references the private records established over the years before — the older the record, the stronger the foundation.

## VEHICLE DOCTRINE — EARLY STAGES

The 5-Layer Sovereign Safeguard Architecture applies regardless of age. For minors, the sequencing is the same — the guardian/trust builds the layers on behalf of the minor until they have legal capacity.

**For a 16/17-year-old with a driver's license:**

| Layer | Action | Who Executes |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| Layer 0 | Class C license — retain. Always. | Minor holds their own |
| Layer 1 | Status correction foundation (private records) | Guardian establishes now |
| Layer 2 | Passport — DS-11 (first adult passport at 16+) | Minor applies with guardian support |
| Layer 3 | Vehicle titled to family trust/LLC | Guardian's trust/LLC holds title |
| Layer 4 | Private motor carrier documentation | Trust/LLC registered as carrier |
| Layer 5 | ARNA private conveyance | ARNA body politic membership established |

**Vehicle acquisition — early stages:**

| Method | How | Pros |
|--------|-----|------|
| Cash purchase through trust | Trust EIN buys vehicle, trust holds title | Cleanest — no personal liability, no personal loan |
| Cash purchase through LLC | LLC EIN buys, LLC holds title | Clean — removes personal name entirely |
| Car payments with personal guarantee | Minor or guardian personally guarantees, vehicle titled to LLC | Workable — LLC on title, personal credit building |
| Business auto loan | LLC with 2+ years history applies directly | Strongest — no personal guarantee needed; requires LLC age |

**Early stage reality:** A new LLC has no credit history, so a direct business auto loan isn't available at formation. The path is: personal guarantee → vehicle titled to LLC/trust → build entity credit history → refinance to business-only loan at 2-year mark. This gets the vehicle into the entity immediately, even if the loan still has a personal guarantee temporarily.
