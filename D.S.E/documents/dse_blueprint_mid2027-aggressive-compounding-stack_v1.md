```

    0% Puerto Rico capital gains tax on investments made AFTER becoming resident

    Requirement: 183+ days/year on the island
    Annual payment: $5,000 to a Puerto Rico charity

ECONOMICS AT MID-2027:
  Current effective rate (federal): 21% corporate + state

  Savings on $200K income: $34,000/year+
  Break-even: rent + lifestyle in PR vs. tax savings
  At mid-2027 income levels: Act 60 becomes highly compelling

SETUP COST:
```
