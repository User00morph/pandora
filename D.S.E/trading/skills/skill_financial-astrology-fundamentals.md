## THE THREE-LEVEL FRAMEWORK

### LEVEL 1 — MACROCOSMIC (Outer Planets)
**Planets:** Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto
**Cycle length:** Years to generations
**What they define:** THE REGIME — the game being played, the rules of the era

```
Saturn:  Structure, limitation, discipline. Saturn transits = regime-defining constraints.
         Saturn sign = the TYPE of discipline being imposed on markets/society.
         
Uranus:  Revolution, disruption, breakthrough. Uranus transits = paradigm shifts.
         Uranus sign = WHERE the revolution is manifesting (Gemini = information/comms).
         
Neptune: Dissolution, illusion, idealism. Neptune transits = narrative dominance.
         Neptune sign = the dominant cultural narrative (Aries = new heroic myths).
         
Pluto:   Transformation, power, death/rebirth at the deepest level.
         Pluto sign = the domain of fundamental power restructuring (Aquarius = collective systems).
```

---

### LEVEL 2 — MESOCOSMIC (Inner/Social Planets)
**Planets:** Jupiter, Mars, Venus, Mercury
**Cycle length:** Weeks to months
**What they define:** THE TONE — the energy quality of a given market period

```
Jupiter: Risk appetite, expansion, optimism. Jupiter transits = bull/bear cycle phases.
         Jupiter in Cancer (2025-2026): safe-haven premium elevated, protection over growth.
         
Mars:    Momentum, aggression, volatility. Mars transits = market tempo.
         Mars direct = momentum building. Mars retrograde = momentum reversal.
         
Venus:   Valuation, currencies, aesthetics. Venus rules currency relationships.
         Venus retrograde = major currency trend reversals likely.
         
Mercury: Information, communication, news flow. Mercury retrograde = confusion/reversals.
         Mercury in home sign (Gemini, Virgo) = amplified market communication speed.
```

---

### LEVEL 3 — MICROCOSMIC (Sun, Moon, Nodes)
**Cycle length:** Daily to weekly
**What they define:** THE TIMING — when within a trend to act

```
Moon:    The most operationally immediate cycle (28-day cycle, sign changes every 2.5 days).
         New Moon = initiation energy, fresh starts.
         Full Moon = culmination, potential reversal zone.
         Moon sign = collective emotional tone for that day/period.
         
Sun:     Monthly cycle through zodiac signs.
         Sign transitions = 3-5 day volatility upticks as energy field shifts.
         
Nodes:   The karmic axis — North Node = collective evolutionary direction.
         Major transits to Nodes = historically significant market inflection points.
```

## HOW TO READ THE CURRENT SKY (daily check)

```
STEP 1 — OUTER PLANET CONTEXT (weekly, check Sunday)
  What signs are Pluto, Neptune, Uranus, Saturn in?
  What is the dominant macro regime? (see skill_outer-planet-cycles.md)
  Are any outer planets changing sign or stationing retrograde/direct soon?

STEP 2 — INNER PLANET TONE (daily check)
  What is Jupiter doing? (risk appetite context)
  Is Mars direct or retrograde? (momentum direction)
  Is Venus stationing? (currency trend warning)
  Is Mercury in its home sign or retrograde? (information speed)

STEP 3 — MOON AND SUN TIMING (daily)
  What sign is the Moon in today? (collective emotional tone)
  How many days until New Moon / Full Moon? (cycle phase)
  Is the Sun changing signs within 3 days? (volatility watch)

STEP 4 — ACTIVE ASPECTS (daily)
  What exact aspects are forming or separating today? (within 1° orb)
  Harmonious (trine/sextile) = smoother price action
  Tense (square/opposition) = volatility, conflict, whipsaw
  Conjunction = amplification (could go either way)
```
