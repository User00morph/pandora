## WHAT THIS IS

A weekly astrology read provides the esoteric timing context for the trading week — identifying days likely to be favorable (harmonious, directional), volatile (chaotic, high-impact), or transitional (regime changes). This is Layer 4 of the STIS — it modifies position sizing and session alertness, it does not generate entries on its own.

## THE DAILY SNAPSHOT (for each trading day)

```
For each day, identify:
  1. MOON SIGN AND HOUSE (check early morning, ~07:00 UTC)
     → Virgo = analytical, health-focused, detail-oriented collective
     → Libra = balance-seeking, relationship themes, potential volatility
     → Leo = attention-seeking, dramatic, risk-on energy
     → Scorpio = intensity, secrets revealed, potential sharp moves
     → Pisces = fluid, confused, low clarity on market direction
  
  2. KEY ASPECTS (exact aspects within 1° orb)
     Harmonious: trine (120°), sextile (60°)
     Tense: square (90°), opposition (180°)
     Major: conjunction (0°) — can be either, depends on planets
  
  3. HOUSE ACTIVATION (what domain is active today?)
     Use skill_house-activation-market-map.md for reference
     2nd house strong = financial/money themes
     6th house strong = work/health/operational themes
     10th house strong = government/authority/major announcements
```

## THE MARKET IMPLICATION FRAMEWORK

```
FAVORABLE (consider full size on confirmed setups):
  → Moon in air signs (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius) trine Mercury or Venus
  → Venus in 2nd house making harmonious aspects
  → Jupiter prominent in favorable aspects
  → Mercury direct in home sign (Gemini, Virgo)

NEUTRAL (standard approach):
  → Moon changing signs (transition day, less predictable)
  → No major aspects exact
  → Normal planetary movement

HIGH-ALERT / VOLATILE (reduce size, wait for clarity):
  → Moon conjunct Mars or Pluto
  → Moon in 7th house squaring other planets (confrontation energy)
  → Mercury retrograde or stationing
  → Mars-Uranus aspects (sudden, unexpected moves)
  → Multiple planets changing signs simultaneously
```

## INTEGRATION INTO POSITION SIZING

```
FAVORABLE day + A+ STIS setup:   full size (1.0× standard)
NEUTRAL day + A+ STIS setup:     full size (1.0× standard)
VOLATILE day + A+ STIS setup:    reduced size (0.75× standard)
VOLATILE day + A STIS setup:     half size (0.5× standard) or pass
CHAOTIC day (multiple tensions):  observation only unless Grade A++ setup
```

---

## RULES

- The astro layer MODIFIES sizing — it does not generate entries or override other layers
- A chaotic astro day does not prevent trading — it requires stronger STIS confirmation
- Log the astro brief accuracy weekly: did high-alert days have market volatility?
- The purpose is timing enhancement and psychological preparation — not superstition
