## THE SEVEN RAYS — MARKET SIGNATURES

### RAY 1 — WILL / POWER (Pluto/Vulcan)
**Price signature:** Large decisive impulse candles, minimal wicks, no retracement.
**Market context:** New macro regime initiation, central bank intervention, first BOS out of long range.
**The trap:** False R1 = looks decisive, wicks back fully within 3 candles. Real R1 DOES NOT fully retrace.
**Trading approach:** Do NOT chase. Position BEFORE (in Polarization). R1 creates reference points for future R2/R3 trades.

---

### RAY 2 — LOVE / WISDOM (Jupiter)
**Price signature:** Clean pullbacks to OBs/FVGs/support. Price gravitates to equilibrium. Volume decreases on pullbacks.
**Market context:** Pullbacks within confirmed Sweep phase. Gold bull markets. Safe-haven flows. Accumulation phases.
**Confirmation signals:** Pullback stays in discount zone, rejection candle at equilibrium, FVG partially filled.
**Trading approach:** PRIMARY TRADING RAY for the Sovereign. The OB return and FVG fill+rejection ARE R2 expressions. Stop below OB/FVG, target prior high.

---

### RAY 3 — ACTIVE INTELLIGENCE (Saturn/Earth)
**Price signature:** Range-bound, choppy, multi-directional. Multiple false breakouts. Algorithm-driven repetitive patterns.
**Market context:** Polarization phase. Ranging DXY. FOMC meeting weeks. Earnings season. Saturn transits.
**The trap:** Trying to trade R3 directionally. R3 is designed to confuse direction.
**Trading approach:** REDUCE SIZE. Wait or scalp range extremes. Watch for the R3 compression that signals R1 approach (the coiling spring).

---

### RAY 4 — HARMONY THROUGH CONFLICT (Mercury)
**Price signature:** Large wicks both directions. Doji candles. Double tops/bottoms. Whipsaw. Climax candles.
**Market context:** Crisis phase. VIX spikes. Post-FOMC whipsaw. Mercury retrograde. 2022-style coordinated decline.
**The R4→R1/R2 transition:** The Volume Climax + Reversal pattern. Maximum conflict → decisive new direction. THE highest-probability moment.
**Trading approach:** REDUCE ALL SIZE. R4 is the energy of the STOP. Watch for the climax candle = the point where R4 has fully expressed. ENTER AFTER the climax.

---

### RAY 5 — CONCRETE KNOWLEDGE (Venus)
**Price signature:** Measured moves. Fibonacci-precise retracements. Technical analysis "working perfectly."
**Market context:** Venus direct in strong sign. Post-crisis technical recovery. When markets are responding to pure logic.
**Trading approach:** Trust the levels. The chart's technical structure is highly reliable during R5 periods.

---

### RAY 6 — DEVOTION / IDEALISM (Neptune/Mars)
**Price signature:** Trend persistence beyond rational levels. Narrative-driven moves. Parabolic rises or crashes.
**Market context:** Neptune in Aries (current). Crypto manias. "This time is different" markets. USD's reserve status narrative.
**The trap:** R6 creates the most dangerous trend extensions — the move goes further than anyone expects.
**Trading approach:** Hold winning R6 trades longer than feels comfortable. The narrative sustains beyond the technical.

---

### RAY 7 — CEREMONIAL ORDER (Uranus)
**Price signature:** Price moving according to the calendar — FOMC ritual, expiration dates, quarterly cycles.
**Market context:** Uranus in Gemini (current). End-of-month/quarter rebalancing. OPEX. The "ritual calendar" behavior of DXY.
**Trading approach:** Know the ritual calendar. The R7 move happens ON the date, not around it.
