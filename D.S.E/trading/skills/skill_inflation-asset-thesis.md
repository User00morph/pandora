## WHAT THIS IS

The systematic trend-following approach works not because of statistical arbitrage or prediction — it works because of a structural macro force: sustained fiat currency debasement. Owning real assets and owing dollars is structurally advantaged because the debt erodes while the assets hold value.

---

## THE FOUNDATION

```
M2 MONEY SUPPLY (US, since 1959): straight up with no sustained reversal.
The one exception: a brief contraction in 2022 → immediately followed by
the sharpest market recovery in decades.

CANTILLON EFFECT:
  Those closest to money printing (governments, banks, institutions) 
  benefit first and most from each new dollar created.
  Those furthest from printing (everyone else) see purchasing power erode.
  
  The response: own assets that benefit from the printing. The printing itself
  becomes the wind in your sails.
```

## THE SOVEREIGN TRADE THESIS

```
WHAT YOU OWN:     Real assets — stocks, gold, silver, Bitcoin, real estate
WHAT YOU OWE:     Dollars (debt, futures margin, mortgage)
WHAT HAPPENS:     The dollar becomes worth less over time
                  → The debt erodes in real terms
                  → The assets appreciate in nominal terms
                  → Net position improves over any long enough horizon

THE FORMULA:
  Own real assets + owe dollars = structurally long the debasement trade
  
HISTORICAL PROOF (extreme):
  Post-WWI Germany: those who borrowed max and bought real assets became wealthy
  Venezuela: same pattern
  Argentina: same pattern (inflation 30-40% per year for decades)
  These aren't unusual — they're the accelerated version of what happens everywhere
```

## THE THESIS HEALTH CHECK (run monthly)

```
□ Is M2 money supply still expanding (or not contracting persistently)?
  YES → thesis intact
  NO  (persistent contraction) → reduce leverage, hold core positions only

□ Are real assets generally trending upward over 12+ months?
  YES → thesis intact
  NO (coordinated decline in gold, stocks, and Bitcoin) → temporary or structural?

□ Is inflation still above 2%?
  YES → the debasement pressure continues
  NO (sustained deflation) → rare, but would challenge the thesis

□ Are central banks still expanding their balance sheets?
  YES → direct money printing continues
  NO  (persistent QT) → thesis faces headwind, reduce leverage
```

## PORTFOLIO IMPLICATIONS

```
THESIS INTACT (all checks pass):
  → Deploy full position sizes
  → Use leverage at calibrated level
  → Hold through normal drawdowns with confidence
  → The macro wind is at your back

THESIS CHALLENGED (1-2 checks fail):
  → Reduce leverage to 50% of normal
  → Hold only tier 1 assets (gold, S&P 500)
  → Do not add new positions until thesis clarifies

THESIS BROKEN (3+ checks fail):
  → This is extremely rare — has occurred only briefly (2022 briefly)
  → Exit leveraged positions, hold cash + gold
  → Re-evaluate within 90 days
```
