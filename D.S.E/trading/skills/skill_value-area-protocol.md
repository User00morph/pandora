# SKILL — VALUE AREA PROTOCOL
**VAH / VAL / POC Mechanics and Market Behavior**
**Load when:** Reading volume profile, identifying key levels, planning entries and exits.
**Department:** D.S.E trading workspace | STIS Layer 2 (Collective Consciousness)

---

## WHAT THIS IS

The value area is where institutions did their business. Understanding how price behaves at VAH, VAL, and POC converts volume data from a visual curiosity into an actionable level system.

---

## THE THREE LEVELS

### POC — Point of Control
The single highest-volume price level in the session. Where the most two-sided trading occurred. Institutions' collective cost basis. **Price gravitates to the POC — bounces, retests, and holds here repeatedly.**

### VAH — Value Area High
The upper boundary of the zone containing 70% of session volume. Acts as resistance on first approach from below. When price breaks above the VAH with volume → initiation signal.

### VAL — Value Area Low
The lower boundary of the same 70% zone. Acts as support on first approach from above. When price breaks below the VAL with volume → initiation signal.

---

## THE 70% CALCULATION

Starting at the POC, expand outward row by row adding whichever adjacent row has higher volume — until 70% of total session volume is captured. The zone between the outermost rows = value area.

---

## PRICE BEHAVIOR AT EACH LEVEL

| Scenario | Expected behavior |
|---|---|
| Price approaches POC from above | Support, bounce, potential retest |
| Price approaches POC from below | Resistance, stall, potential rejection |
| Price at VAH (from below) | Resistance — watch for rejection or breakout |
| Price breaks above VAH with volume | INITIATION — institutions loaded inside, now pushing out |
| Price at VAL (from above) | Support — watch for hold or breakdown |
| Price breaks below VAL with volume | INITIATION — bearish extension likely |

---

## THE INITIATION SIGNAL

**"Institutions loaded inside the range before pushing out."**

When price breaks outside the value area with above-average volume, institutions have finished accumulation and are initiating a move. This is the highest-probability entry zone in the volume profile system.

**Entry structure:** Break of VAH/VAL → retest of the broken level → hold → entry. The broken level flips from resistance to support (or vice versa).

---

## CONNECTION TO GEX DAILY MODEL

The value area breakout is the volume profile equivalent of the GEX daily model entry sequence:
- Initiation (break of VAH/VAL) = GEX level break
- Retest = return to GEX level
- Hold = value build confirmation

When GEX levels and value area levels coincide → Grade A++ setup.

---

## RULES

- POC is a magnet, not just support/resistance — price will return to it
- Value area breakout requires volume confirmation — low-volume breaks are traps
- The prior session's value area is more reliable than the same session's developing value area
