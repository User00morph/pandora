# SKILL — GEX LEVEL HIERARCHY
**What Each GEX Level Is and How to Use It Correctly**
**Load when:** Building the daily level stack, reviewing GEX output, any GEX-related decision.
**Department:** D.S.E trading workspace | STIS Layer 1b | Level map

---

## WHAT THIS IS

Five distinct GEX level types. Each has a different mechanical meaning and must be used differently. Treating all of them as "support and resistance" is the #1 misuse — and why most people's GEX setups stop working.

---

## THE FIVE LEVEL TYPES

### 1. CALL RESISTANCE / PUT SUPPORT
**What it is:** Where the heaviest call/put open interest sits across ALL expiries.
**Mechanical meaning:** Ceiling (call wall) and floor (put wall) where dealer hedging creates maximum opposing force.
**How to use:** Weekly/monthly range boundaries. Don't move much day to day. NOT daily S/R.
**Misuse:** Treating as intraday support/resistance. These are structural anchors, not tactical levels.

---

### 2. HVL — HIGH VOLATILITY LEVEL
**What it is:** The strike where gamma flips from positive to negative.
**Mechanical meaning:**
- Above HVL → dealers long gamma → DAMPEN moves (mean reversion)
- Below HVL → dealers short gamma → AMPLIFY moves (trending)
**How to use:** Regime classifier only. Tells you the behavioral template for the day.
**Misuse:** Treating as a price target. Also: relying on it solely — macro can override (positive gamma + massive rally still happens).

---

### 3. GAMMA WALL
**What it is:** The single strike with the LARGEST positive gamma concentration.
**Mechanical meaning:** The strongest version of call resistance. Price stalls, bounces, and consolidates around it with maximum force.
**How to use:** The most reliable ceiling in the level stack. When price approaches the gamma wall, dealer hedging resistance is at maximum intensity.
**Misuse:** Confusing it with call resistance. The gamma wall is more extreme — it's the peak, not the average.

---

### 4. ZERO DTE STACK
**What it is:** The same structure (call resistance, put support, HVL, gamma wall) calculated ONLY from same-day expiry options.
**Mechanical meaning:** Since 2022, zero DTE = **40-50% of total options volume**. These levels are tighter, more reactive, and often more precise than all-expiry levels for intraday action.
**How to use:** The precision layer on top of all-expiry. When zero DTE and all-expiry levels align → maximum confluence. When they diverge → use zero DTE for intraday, all-expiry for swing.
**Misuse:** Ignoring them entirely or treating all-expiry as sufficient for intraday.

---

### 5. GEX 1-10
**What it is:** Top 10 strikes ranked by absolute gamma exposure. GEX 1 = heaviest dealer activity.
**Mechanical meaning:** Levels where the most dealer hedging occurs. Price gravitates to and reacts at these strikes repeatedly.
**How to use:** TARGETS and CONFIRMATION only. Never build a trade narrative from GEX levels. Build narrative from call/put walls and gamma wall — use GEX 1-10 to set exits and targets.
**Misuse:** Using GEX levels as entries. They are destinations, not reasons to enter.

---

## THE "NO GEX ABOVE" SIGNAL

When there is **no gamma exposure above a key level:**
- If that level breaks → **vacuum move**
- No dealer hedging overhead → price runs freely
- Violent break of a concentrated level with nothing above = **major bullish signal**

Conversely: dense GEX levels stacked above = strong ceiling, expect resistance or stall.

---

## LEVEL HIERARCHY SUMMARY

```
STRUCTURAL (weekly/monthly):   Call Resistance → Gamma Wall → Put Support
REGIME (daily):                HVL (above = range, below = trend)
PRECISION (intraday):          Zero DTE stack
TARGETS (exits):               GEX 1-10
```
