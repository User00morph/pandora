**The Mechanical Cause of All Price Movement**

## WHAT THIS IS

Options flow — not order blocks, not liquidity sweeps, not retail stop hunting — is the actual mechanical cause of futures price movement. Market makers on SPX/QQQ/NDX options must hedge on ES/NQ futures. That hedging IS what you see on the tape.

---

## THE DEALER OPPOSITION TABLE

Burn this into memory. Dealers always do the opposite of your position to stay Delta neutral.

| Your action | Dealer exposure | Dealer hedge action |
|---|---|---|
| Buy call | Negative Delta | BUY NQ/ES futures |
| Buy put | Positive Delta | SELL NQ/ES futures |
| Sell call | Positive Delta | SELL NQ/ES futures |
| Sell put | Negative Delta | BUY NQ/ES futures |

**Master principle:** Dealers are always trying to be Delta neutral. Every time price moves, volatility moves, or time passes — they re-hedge. Continuous. Mechanical. No emotion. No stop hunting.

---

## THE FLOW PIPELINE

```
Options buyer places order
        ↓
Market maker sells the option (takes the other side)
        ↓
Market maker is now exposed to directional risk
        ↓
Market maker immediately buys/sells NQ/ES futures to offset
        ↓
That futures buying/selling IS the price movement you see
```

This happens every day for billions of dollars. It is not random. It is not emotional. It is mechanical.
