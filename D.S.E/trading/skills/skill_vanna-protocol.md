## WHAT THIS IS

Vanna is the force that moves NQ/ES when VIX moves — invisible on the chart, invisible on order flow, but mechanically real. It creates the "mystery drift" that confuses discretionary traders.

---

## THE MECHANISM

```
VIX DECLINING:
  Implied volatility decreasing
  → Dealer Delta recalculates lower
  → Dealers have EXCESS hedge (over-hedged)
  → They BUY underlying to rebalance
  → Mechanical long tailwind in background

VIX RISING:
  Implied volatility increasing
  → Dealer Delta recalculates higher
  → Dealers are UNDERHEDGED
  → They SELL underlying to rebalance
  → Mechanical short headwind in background
```

**The invisible drift pattern:** VIX spikes → NQ drops hard → everyone panics → a few days later VIX fades down slowly → NQ starts drifting higher with no news, no catalyst, no buyer visible on tape. That drift IS Vanna. Dealers mechanically buying as VIX unwinds.

"That is one thing you can see neither on order flow nor on the chart." — only visible if you know it exists.

---

## THE MORNING RULE

**Check VIX FIRST every morning. Before NQ. Before the chart. Before anything.**

```
VIX DECLINING (5-day trend):
  → Long bias baked in mechanically
  → Fades against the drift will fail repeatedly
  → Don't fight the invisible buyer

VIX RISING (5-day trend):
  → Mechanical selling pressure on all longs
  → Rallies feel weak, fade fast, no follow-through
  → Cautious on longs — reduce size or skip

VIX FLAT (±0.5 over 3 days):
  → Vanna neutral
  → Gamma regime is the dominant force today
```

---

## THE DIAGNOSTIC

When a move looks unexplained — no news, no catalyst, no clear order flow — **Vanna is the first answer.** Check the 5-day VIX trend before concluding the move is random.
