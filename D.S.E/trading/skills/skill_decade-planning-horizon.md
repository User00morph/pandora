# SKILL — DECADE PLANNING HORIZON
**Using 10-Year Timeframes as the Correct Performance Unit**
**Load when:** During a losing period, when doubting the system, or setting annual expectations.
**Department:** D.S.E trading workspace | STIS Observer Layer | Long-horizon calibration

---

## WHAT THIS IS

Systematic trend-following systems have won 100% of historical decades in backtests covering 60+ years. A losing year is not a system failure — it is a normal data point inside a 10-year sequence that has always been positive. This skill provides the frame to hold that perspective during real drawdowns.

---

## THE DECADE FRAME

```
HISTORICAL DECADE PERFORMANCE (MTP system, multi-asset portfolio):
  Every 10-year period tested: POSITIVE
  Average decade return: ~22,000% (approximately 20× starting capital)
  Worst decade: 300-400% (still 3-4× starting capital)
  Best decade: driven by whichever asset had the decade's big trend
  
DISTRIBUTION:
  Winning years within each decade: 70-77% (not 100%)
  This means: 2-3 losing years per decade is NORMAL
  One losing year does not predict the decade outcome
```

---

## THE REFRAME PROTOCOL

When a losing period triggers doubt, run this mental sequence:

```
STEP 1 — Identify the current position
  "I am in [Year N] of a decade. The decade started in [Year]."

STEP 2 — Check the decade-level data
  "Every decade in 60+ years of backtest history was positive."
  "I am not in a losing decade. I am in a losing year inside a likely-winning decade."

STEP 3 — Check if the loss is within normal variance
  "The expected losing year in this system averages -11 to -13%."
  "My current drawdown is [X]%. Is this within the normal losing year range?"
  If yes → this is EXPECTED VARIANCE, not system failure
  If no (loss exceeds worst historical year) → investigate, do not automatically hold

STEP 4 — Check the system's thesis
  "Is the fundamental reason this system works still true?"
  "Is money supply still expanding? Are real assets still in long-run demand?"
  If yes → the decade-level thesis is intact. Continue.
```

---

## THE COMMUNICATION PROTOCOL

When asked "how is the trading going?" during a losing year:

"The system has a 30% probability of a losing year in any given year. This is one of those years. The average decade in the backtest has produced 20× returns. I'm holding through the expected variance."

Not: "It's been hard lately." Not: "I might adjust the parameters." Not: "I'm thinking about exiting."

---

## THE ZOOMING PROTOCOL

When looking at a distressing chart:

```
ZOOM OUT:
  If viewing daily → switch to weekly
  If viewing weekly → switch to monthly
  If viewing monthly → switch to yearly
  
The longer the timeframe, the clearer the underlying trend appears.
The discomfort of drawdowns is always largest on the shortest timeframe.
```

---

## RULES

- The planning horizon for this system is decades, not years
- A losing year triggers a thesis check, not a system change
- System parameters are not adjusted based on recent performance — they are adjusted based on new data in the walk-forward optimization, run on a fixed schedule
