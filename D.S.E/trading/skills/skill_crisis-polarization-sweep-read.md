## THE THREE PHASES

### PHASE 1 — CRISIS
```
WHAT IT IS:
  The existing equilibrium breaks.
  Tension exceeds the structure's capacity to hold it.
  Multiple contradictory narratives compete.
  
PRICE SIGNATURES:
  → Expanding volatility — ATR climbing
  → Multiple failed breakouts in both directions
  → Large wicks on both sides of price
  → No clean higher highs OR lower lows — structural ambiguity
  → Volume spikes on both directions without sustained follow-through
  → News cycle is incoherent — conflicting reports
  
RAY DOMINANT: R4 (Harmony through Conflict)
PARTICIPANT ACTIVITY: Mutable Cross in maximum pain/confusion; Fixed Cross being forced to unwind
  
SOVEREIGN ACTION: OBSERVE ONLY. Do not take new positions.
                  Crisis is not the entry — it is the prerequisite for the entry.
                  The crisis is creating the structural reference points for the next trade.
                  
WHEN TO SUSPECT CRISIS: Previous trend has broken, structure is ambiguous, ATR elevated
```

---

### PHASE 2 — POLARIZATION
```
WHAT IT IS:
  From chaos, a new dominant force emerges.
  One side begins to win decisively.
  A new structure tentatively forms.
  The CHoCH (Change of Character) is the structural signal Polarization has begun.
  
PRICE SIGNATURES:
  → First CHoCH appears — a BOS against the prior trend that HOLDS
  → Price begins making tentative HH+HL or LL+LH
  → Volume on the new direction is slightly higher than on retreats
  → Volatility begins to contract from Crisis levels
  → A new narrative begins to dominate (even if not fully believed yet)
  
RAY DOMINANT: R3 (Active Intelligence — multiple narratives being processed)
PARTICIPANT ACTIVITY: Cardinal Cross CALIBRATES. Identifies the new direction. Forms the hypothesis.
  
SOVEREIGN ACTION: CALIBRATE. Do not rush to enter.
                  Identify the new dominant direction from the CHoCH.
                  Build the hypothesis (see skill_hypothesis-formation-protocol.md).
                  The FIRST entry opportunity forms at the END of Polarization.
                  
THE POLARIZATION ENTRY SETUP:
  The CHoCH has formed → a pullback develops → the pullback holds above the CHoCH level
  → THIS is the entry at the end of Polarization, just before the Sweep begins
```

### PHASE 3 — SWEEP FORWARD
```
WHAT IT IS:
  The new force expresses unimpeded.
  Trend is clean. Structure makes consistent HH+HL (bull) or LL+LH (bear).
  Volume is consistent. Price moves with minimal friction.
  
PRICE SIGNATURES:
  → Consistent higher highs and higher lows (bull) OR lower lows and lower highs (bear)
  → Pullbacks are orderly — no failed attempts to reverse
  → ATR contracting from crisis levels — move is controlled, not chaotic
  → Volume on impulses > volume on pullbacks (healthy trend structure)
  → Each pullback is smaller than the prior impulse (trend momentum intact)
  
RAY DOMINANT: R1 (Will/Power on initiation) → R2 (Love/Wisdom on pullbacks)
PARTICIPANT ACTIVITY: Cardinal Cross EXECUTES. Fixed Cross follows. Mutable Cross buys the top/sells the bottom.
  
SOVEREIGN ACTION: EXECUTE.
                  The Sweep is WHERE the edge lives — position EARLY in the Sweep.
                  The objective is to be positioned at the START of the Sweep, not chasing the middle.
                  The R2 pullbacks within the Sweep are the PRIMARY entry opportunities.
```

## THE PHASE IDENTIFICATION PROTOCOL

```
STEP 1 — LOOK AT THE STRUCTURE (on the analysis timeframe)
  □ Are higher highs and higher lows forming consistently? → Sweep
  □ Is price oscillating between two levels with no trend? → Polarization (or pre-Crisis)
  □ Are multiple failed breakouts visible in both directions? → Crisis

STEP 2 — CHECK ATR
  □ ATR expanding rapidly → Crisis
  □ ATR moderately elevated, beginning to contract → Late Crisis / Early Polarization
  □ ATR normal and stable → Sweep (or deep Polarization consolidation)

STEP 3 — LOOK FOR THE CHoCH
  □ Has a Change of Character occurred (first BOS against prior trend that held)?
  □ NO → still in Crisis or early Polarization
  □ YES → Polarization confirmed → watch for Sweep initiation

STEP 4 — CHECK HIGHER TIMEFRAME
  □ What cycle phase is the daily/weekly chart in?
  □ A Sweep on the weekly timeframe means pullbacks on the daily may LOOK like Crisis
     but are actually R2 opportunities within the larger Sweep
```
