# SKILL — SESSION POC ENTRY MODEL
**Four-Layer Institutional Entry Model with Dynamic Stop Loss**
**Load when:** Evaluating any intraday trade setup using session structure.
**Department:** D.S.E trading workspace | STIS Layer 1+2 confluence

---

## WHAT THIS IS

A four-layer confluence model that fires a signal only when institutional fingerprints align across session structure, volume delta, price action, and GEX. The grade system replaces discretionary "gut feel" with measurable criteria.

---

## THE FOUR LAYERS

### Layer 1 — Session Breakout Confirmed (no fake out)
Price has broken outside the session range (Asia or London box) and held outside it. A fake out = price broke out and immediately returned inside the range. Fake out detected = Layer 1 FAILS.

### Layer 2 — Volume Delta Skewed in Breakout Direction
Buyers must be dominating for long breakouts. Sellers must be dominating for short breakouts. Delta skewed AGAINST the breakout direction = warning sign (potential trap).

### Layer 3 — Breakout Bar Volume Above Average
The breakout candle must show above-average volume. Low-volume breakout = institutional absence = likely failure.

### Layer 4 — Session POC is Behind the Move
The session POC must be BELOW current price for longs (above for shorts). This confirms institutions loaded their position inside the session range BEFORE pushing out. The POC behind the move = institutional accumulation fingerprint.

---

## THE GRADE SYSTEM

| Layers aligned | Grade | Action |
|---|---|---|
| All 4 | A+ | Full size |
| Any 3 | B | Half size |
| Any 2 | C | Quarter size or pass |
| 1 or 0 | Rejected | No trade |

---

## THE MSS SIGNAL (Market Structure Shift)

When price makes a strong directional move and then an **MSS label fires** (structure shifts on heavy volume in the opposite direction):
- This is NOT a reversal entry signal
- This is an **early warning** — protect capital on the open position
- "This one label could save you from giving back all your profits on a reversal"

MSS = the indicator's risk management layer, not an entry layer.

---

## DYNAMIC STOP LOSS PLACEMENT

Stop loss goes at the **session POC**, not at a fixed distance:
- Long trades: stop below the session POC
- Short trades: stop above the session POC

The POC is where institutions loaded — if price returns to and breaks through their cost basis, the trade thesis is invalidated.

---

## RULES

- Never enter on a fake out (Layer 1 fail) regardless of other layers
- The POC must be BEHIND the move — not ahead of it
- MSS on an open position = tighten stop or partial exit, not a reversal entry
