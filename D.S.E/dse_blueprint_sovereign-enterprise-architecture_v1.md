**S-Corp mechanics (educator's exact framework — V4, ESTABLISHED tier):**
1. Multi-member LLC receives EIN from IRS
2. File Form 2553 → S-Corp election
3. File Form 1120S annually — no federal income tax at entity level
4. Shareholders receive W-2 (active participation — "reasonable compensation" is an IRS enforcement mechanism — audit target if skipped per IRS Revenue Ruling 74-44) + Schedule K-1
5. K-1 income taxed as ordinary income on shareholder's personal return
6. 95–98% of net profit → K-1 → trust
7. 2–5% → 1099 → family foundation
8. Trust + foundation purchase whole life insurance → tax-free loans drawn against policy

**⚠️ Open question (from D.R.D Stage 2):** If 95–98% goes to trust via K-1 and shareholder also takes W-2, does the W-2 come out of the 2–5% remainder or from gross before the K-1 split? Not addressed by source — structure with CPA before first payroll.
