## The Real Math — Brutal Version

The arithmetic in your post ($10K/account × 5 accounts = $50K/month) is correct. What it leaves out:

- **Only 5–10% of challenge attempts pass**, and roughly **7% of all traders who ever start a challenge end up receiving a payout** ([source](https://www.quantvps.com/blog/prop-firm-statistics)). Passing Phase 1 once is already the hard part.
- **40–50% of funded accounts get breached within the first 90 days** — the psychology changes once losses are real and permanent instead of "just try again" ([source](https://www.quantvps.com/blog/prop-firm-statistics)).
- To run this plan across **5 accounts in parallel**, you're not just clearing one low-probability event — you're clearing it, then not blowing up, five separate times, while managing five accounts' worth of execution discipline at once. That compounds the difficulty, it doesn't average it out.
- The plan assumes payout timing lines up neatly with "$1,600 buys two more evals." In practice payouts run on a cycle (biweekly/first-payout-after-N-days depending on program), so the reinvestment loop is slower than the post implies.

None of this means it's impossible — traders do run multi-account portfolios this way. It means the honest version has a timeline measured in years of consistent execution, not a few good months.

## What You're Missing — Blind Spots

1. **Gold and GBP/JPY aren't a diversified pair, they're a correlated risk position.** Gold is a classic safe-haven; GBP is a risk-sensitive currency; JPY is also a safe-haven. In a sharp risk-off shock (geopolitical event, surprise hawkish Fed, BoJ intervention), gold often spikes *while GBP/JPY sells off hard*. If you're long gold and long GBP/JPY when that hits, both legs can move against a "just markets being normal" assumption at the same time, across all 5 accounts simultaneously if you're holding the same trade everywhere.
2. **FXIFY explicitly bans reverse and group hedging across accounts** — offsetting positions between accounts to game the risk rules is an instant termination offense ([source](https://fxify.com/blog/fxifys-prohibited-strategies/)). Taking the *same* trade direction on 5 accounts isn't hedging, but heavy multi-account, same-IP activity can trigger their KYC review — worth being aware of, not necessarily a blocker.
3. **Phase 1 gives you almost no room for error.** At 3% daily loss / 6% max drawdown on a One-Phase $200K account, two bad days at 2% risk each puts you within striking distance of the daily cap. There's no "trade your way out of a slow start" — a couple of undisciplined days can end the whole attempt.
4. **Reinvestment isn't instant.** New eval fees plus new challenge time (min. 5 trading days, no fixed max) means the "buy 2 more evals with the payout" step adds weeks-to-months per cycle, not days.
5. **Monitoring load scales linearly, discipline doesn't automatically.** Five $200K accounts on two instruments means five times the chances for a fat-finger entry, a missed stop, or one bad emotional day to do real damage — right when the accounts are hardest to replace (funded, not eval).

## The "10X This" Framing

There's no clean way to compress this timeline by increasing risk — the math works against you. At 2% risk per trade during Phase 1, against a 3% daily loss limit, two losers in a row ends the attempt; you don't get faster, you get closer to zero attempts remaining. The only real lever for speed is **more high-quality setups executed with unchanged risk**, not bigger risk per trade. Anyone selling "risk 5% and 10x your timeline" is selling you a faster way to fail the evaluation.

## A+ Setup Criteria — Nawaf EMA Baseline Model + KimG Structure + Session Timing

This is the concrete, mechanical version. An entry is **A+ only if every box is ticked**. Missing one = not A+ = no trade. The point of a mechanical checklist is to remove the "keep clicking" impulse the journal keeps flagging.

**Indicators to load on the chart (all timeframes):** 9 EMA (red), 18 EMA (blue), 50 EMA, 50 SMA. The 50 EMA/50 SMA together are the **"baseline."**

### KimG (The Fx Diva) structure model — the WHERE (extracted from her 26 Oct 2025 session)
This defines the map; Nawaf's steps below define when to pull the trigger on it.
- **Top-down:** set bias/structure on the HTF (Daily/4H). Mark **external (swing) structure** first — swing highs/lows — then drop to **internal (LTF) structure** to find the entry.
- **Only trade after a structure shift:** wait for a break of structure (BOS) / market-structure shift in your direction. An internal shift inside the HTF zone is the cue to start hunting an entry — not before.
- **Zone quality — unmitigated only:** enter from an **unmitigated** demand zone (buys) or supply zone / last opposing candle-order block (sells). If price already tapped the zone (mitigated), it's lower quality — skip or downgrade.
- **Premium/discount refinement (fib):** buy in **discount** (price into the 50% / 61.8% / 78.6% retrace of the leg); sell in **premium**. 38.2%/50% are shallow entries; 61.8–78.6% are the deeper, higher-quality discount.
- **Liquidity = the target/draw:** mark **Asian range high/low and London high/low**. These session liquidity pools are what price is reaching for — use them as the "why" behind the setup and as the profit target.
- **Confirmation, not raw imbalance:** don't trade the naked gap. Wait for price to pull **into the zone → LTF structure shift → candle close** confirming, then act.
- **Mindset (from her session stats, XAUUSD, 6 trades):** Win rate ~33%, Profit Factor 1.27 — profitable *only* because avg win (~$2,383) ≈ **1.9× avg loss** (~$1,249). The edge is **asymmetric R:R, not hit rate.** Let winners run to the liquidity target; keep losers small. This is the direct antidote to the negative profit factor in the journal.
- *Confidence note: extracted from low-res transcript screenshots of one session — framework is solid; refine any specific number/rule if a fuller transcript says otherwise.*

### Step 0 — Context filter (do first, every time)
- [ ] **Session:** acting inside the London or New York killzone (or the overlap). Mark Tokyo/London/NY session boxes. No dead-zone entries.
- [ ] **News:** no entry in the 5-minute window before/after a flagged high-impact USD/GBP/JPY release.
- [ ] **Direction bias (KimG):** HTF structure has **shifted (BOS)** in the trade direction; there is a **fresh unmitigated** demand/supply zone or order block; price is in **discount for buys / premium for sells** (fib 50–78.6%); and there is a clear **liquidity target** (Asian/London high-low) for the move to run into.

### Step 1 — HTF baseline touch (H1 or H2)
- [ ] Price has **physically touched** its 50 EMA, 50 SMA, or daily equilibrium on the higher timeframe. (For a buy, the HTF low reached ≤ the baseline; for a sell, the HTF high reached ≥ the baseline.) **No HTF touch = no setup, stop here.**

### Step 2 — LTF baseline pullback (M15 or M30)
- [ ] The lower timeframe has **also** touched its own 50 EMA/50 SMA. Touch = candle **High ≥ baseline** (for sells) or **Low ≤ baseline** (for buys). Both HTF *and* LTF must touch — extended/expanded price with no pullback is a NO.

### Step 3 — Candle maturity (body close)
- [ ] A **completed candle closes with its full body beyond the 50 EMA** in the trade direction. Wicks piercing the line do **not** count — body close only.

### Step 4 — EMA momentum alignment
- [ ] **The cross:** 9 EMA crosses 18 EMA in the trade direction (9 > 18 for buys, 9 < 18 for sells).
- [ ] **The position:** both the 9 and 18 EMAs sit on the correct side of the 50 EMA (above for buys, below for sells). On the M5, 9/18 must already be stacked on the correct side of the 50 before execution.

### Step 5 — The wick (trigger)
- [ ] The signal candle first makes a **retracement wick against** the trade direction, then **flips back** into the trend. Enter on the flip, not before.

### Stop & target (ties to KimG structure + risk caps)
- [ ] **Stop** goes beyond the structural invalidation — past the far edge of the unmitigated order block / swing low (buys) or swing high (sells), *and* respects the size caps: Gold stop ≤ 30 pts (else cut size or skip); GJ only when intervention risk is not live.
- [ ] **Target = KimG liquidity draw:** the Asian/London session high or low, opposing order block, or next structure level. Because the edge is asymmetric R:R, size the first target for **at least ~1.9× the risk** (mirrors her avg-win : avg-loss); scale out across 1–3 TPs and let a runner reach the liquidity pool.

### Non-negotiables (unchanged, they override everything above)
- One open position at a time. Never two, never averaging in.
- Max 2 trades/day. After any loss → flat, no re-entry that session.
- Limit/stop orders only fill the plan; if price passes the level first, the trade no longer exists — no market chases.
- If you can't articulate the setup in one sentence naming the session, the baseline touch, and the trigger, it isn't A+.

**Summary checklist (say all four out loud before entry):** HTF touched 50? · LTF touched 50? · Body close past 50? · 9/18 stacked on the right side + wick-flip trigger?

## Recommended Path — Sequential, Highest-Probability

No version of this guarantees success. This is the sequencing that gives the best odds, based on where most traders actually fail (funded, first 90 days) rather than where the viral post assumes the risk is (passing Phase 1).

**Stage 0 — Foundation (before risking the eval fee)**
- Backtest or demo your A+ Gold/GBP-JPY criteria until you have a real sample (win rate, average R:R) — not a feeling.
- Build the trade journal template now, not after you're funded.

**Stage 1 — Pass Phase 1 ($200K, account #1)**
- 2% risk/trade, A+ setups only, target 10%, protect the 3% daily / 6% drawdown limits above the profit target.
- Budget for 2–3 attempts financially and mentally — 5–10% is the per-attempt pass rate industry-wide.

**Stage 2 — Funded, Probation (Months 1–3, account #1 only)**
- Drop to 1% risk/trade. Target 5%/month, but treat "didn't breach the limits" as the actual win condition — this window is where 40–50% of funded accounts fail.
- Do not buy account #2 yet, no matter how good month 1 feels.

**Stage 3 — First Scale Gate (month 3 checkpoint)**
- Buy account #2 only if all three are true: 10% cumulative return, 2 of 3 months profitable, and your journal shows you actually followed the A+ checklist (not just that you made money).
- If any of the three is false: stay on one account, fix the process, don't add capital to an unproven system.

**Stage 4 — Sequential Multiplication (months 3–12+)**
- Add one account at a time, each gated by its own 3-month proof checkpoint. Realistic cadence: account #2 around month 3–6, #3 around month 6–9, #4 around month 9–12.
- This is slower than "5 accounts fast," but each account only gets added once the prior process is proven — the failures don't compound across all five at once.

**Stage 5 — Steady State ($50K/month)**
- Once 5 accounts are independently funded and each clearing ~5%/month, you're at the target run rate. Realistic timeline on this gated path: 18–30+ months, not a few months.

|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | Month 0–3 (maybe 2–3 attempts) | 1 eval | Pass without breaching limits |
| Probation | Month 1–3 (post-pass) | 1 funded | 10% cumulative, 2/3 months profitable |

## "10X This" — What Actually Compresses the Timeline

Risk can't be the lever — it already fails against the 3%/6% limits (covered above). The honest levers, in order of impact:

1. **Setup frequency without lowering the bar.** More clean A+ setups per week (not more marginal ones) shortens the road to the 10%/5% targets. This comes from screen time and pattern recognition, not from search harder for reasons to enter.
2. **Faster feedback loops.** A rigorous weekly review (Friday step above) catches bad habits in week 2 instead of month 4 — this is probably the single biggest real-world accelerant.
3. **Program choice, later.** FXIFY's Lightning program pays out faster (first payout ~7 days) but has a tighter 4% drawdown and no EAs/news trading — worth considering once your process is proven, not as a starting point, since the tighter limits punish an unproven system faster.
4. **Full reinvestment, once proven.** Once Stage 3's gate is genuinely met, reinvesting 100% of payouts into new evals (rather than withdrawing) is what actually funds the sequential scale-up.

Realistic compression: a disciplined trader can likely pull the 18–30 month timeline into 12–18 months by tightening steps 1–2. Nothing legitimately compresses it into "a few months across 5 accounts" — that's the part of the original post that isn't the math, it's the marketing.
