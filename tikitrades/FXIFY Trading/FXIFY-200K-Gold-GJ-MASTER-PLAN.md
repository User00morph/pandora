## MANAGING THE TRADE — partials, breakeven, TP (ELI5)

You're in the trade. Now what. This part is mechanical so there's no thinking in the moment.

**ELI5:** When the trade goes your way and hits the first flag (TP1), you scoop up **half the money and put it in your pocket**, then you **slide your safety net up to where you got in** so the trade can never bite you again. The other half you let run to the far flags. If it turns around, you get out at zero — you already banked half. Heads you win, tails you don't lose.

**Exact splits (memorize these):**

| Pair | Total size | Close at TP1 | Runner to TP2/TP3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gold** | 0.33 lots | **0.17** | 0.16 |
| **GBP/JPY** | 2.00 lots | **1.00** | 1.00 |

**Breakeven — the exact number:** the second TP1 fills, move the stop on the runner to **your exact entry price** (not a tick away — the price you got in at). That's your breakeven. Today's live examples from the coordinates:

- Gold LONG entry 4,002 → after TP1, stop to **4,002**
- Gold SHORT entry 4,038 → after TP1, stop to **4,038**
- Gold SHORT (break) entry 3,998 → after TP1, stop to **3,998**
- GJ SHORT entry 218.60 → after TP1, stop to **218.60**

(Rule, not those numbers: **BE stop = whatever price you actually entered at.**)

**When is TP1?** TP1 = **1R** — the same distance as your entry-to-stop, measured up (long) or down (short). If your stop is 17 pts away, TP1 is 17 pts in profit. Close 0.17 gold / 1.00 GJ there, stop to breakeven, done deciding.

**Moving a TP (only for a reason, never for "more room"):**
1. **Default: don't touch it.** The target was set calmly; don't undo it because it's going well.
2. **Trail only on strong momentum** — price rips *through* T with big bodies and no wicks → trail the runner behind the new higher-low (long) / lower-high (short).
3. **Pull it in if it stalls** — long rejection wicks and dying momentum into the target → take profit early, before the level.
4. **Respect a wall just before T** — if resistance sits under your target, take it at the wall.

The read at every target is the same three things: **momentum** (accelerating or fading?), the **candle** (pushing through or wicking off?), the **level** (S/R right there?). Through + strong = trail. Fade + wick + level = take it now.

> Why half-off matters for *you specifically:* your $5,133 drawdown room is the real wall, and your last three sessions died *holding for more*. Banking the 0.17/1.00 at TP1 + breakeven means the trade can never turn into a loss. Take the money.
