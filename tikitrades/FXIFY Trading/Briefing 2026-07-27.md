## 1. What happened this weekend — the regime shift (ELI5)
**The news:** the US and Iran **halted strikes** and are moving toward **negotiations** (with China and Pakistan helping broker it). Result at the Sunday open: **Brent crude oil crashed 7–10%**, stock futures **surged**, and gold **gapped up to ~4,090**.

**Why gold went UP when a war calming down should send it DOWN — the key insight:**
Two forces act on gold, and they usually fight:
- **Fear/war premium** → when scary, money hides in gold (pushes gold **up**). De-escalation removes this → *bearish* gold.
- **Interest-rate expectations** → gold pays no interest, so when rates are expected to *rise*, gold gets less attractive (pushes gold **down**). 

For weeks, gold was **capped** because oil was spiking → inflation fear → markets priced an **80% chance of a September Fed hike**. That rate-hike fear was the lid on gold.

**Oil just crashed 7–10%.** That **destroys the inflation-hike story.** So the *lid comes off* gold (bullish) — and that rate-relief turned out to be **bigger than the lost war premium.** Net: **gold gapped up.**

**One-line takeaway:** the market decided *"cheaper oil = no Fed hike"* matters more than *"war's over, sell the safe-haven."* That's a **gold-friendly regime**, as long as it holds.

**Knock-on for this week:** with oil collapsing, the **Fed has far less reason to sound hawkish on Wednesday.** The scary "hawkish hold" scenario just got less likely — another mild tailwind for gold. But "less likely" isn't "gone" — you still don't hold through the event (see §5).

## 2. The gap — what it is and how to treat it (ELI5)
A **gap** is when price *jumps* over the weekend instead of trading smoothly — Friday closed ~**4,053**, Sunday opened ~**4,085–4,090**. That ~35-point jump with no candles in between is the gap.

Two things to know about gaps:
- **Gaps often "fill."** Price frequently drifts back to where it jumped from (~4,053) to trade the empty zone, *then* decides. So an up-gap is **not** an automatic "buy."
- **Gap-and-go** is the other outcome: if the news is strong enough, price holds the gap and keeps going, never looking back.

**And notice where it gapped TO:** straight into the **4,086–4,093 resistance/order-block** — the same ceiling that rejected you all Friday. So gold **gapped into a wall.** That's the definition of "don't chase the open."

**The two paths this week:**
- **Gap-and-go (bullish):** price *holds above 4,090* → targets 4,120 → 4,150 → 4,200.
- **Gap-fill (neutral):** price *rejects 4,090* and fades back toward 4,053 (or lower) → back inside the box.

You don't guess which. You let **4,090** tell you.
